# Drama Deck Initiative (module)

_Used when `campaign.md` sets `initiative: drama-deck`. Torg-style side initiative: each round you flip one Drama Deck card that says which side acts first and what advantage or disadvantage applies. Individual initiative rolls are not used._

## How a round works

1. **Tag the scene** when combat starts: *Standard* (routine fights, favors heroes) or *Dramatic* (boss, ambush, or story-critical fights, favors villains). Say which in the status block.
2. **Flip a card** at the top of each round and announce it openly, e.g. `Card 7 — Standard: Heroes first, Villains Stymied`. Read only the line matching the scene tag.
3. **The first side acts in full**, then the other side. Players choose their own order within the side; you choose the villains' order.
4. **Effects last for that round only** unless the card says otherwise.

## Card effects

| Effect | Type | What it does in 5e |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flurry | Advantage | The side takes two full turns back-to-back before the other side acts. |
| Up | Advantage | Advantage on the first attack roll or saving throw each creature makes this round. |
| Inspiration | Advantage | Each PC gains Heroic Inspiration or ends one condition on themselves (villains: regain HP equal to their proficiency bonus). |
| Stymied | Disadvantage | No rerolls this round: no Heroic Inspiration, Lucky, Indomitable, or similar. |
| Confused | Disadvantage | No reactions this round. |
| Fatigued | Disadvantage | Each creature on the side takes 1d4 damage that ignores resistance at the start of its turn. |
| Setback | Disadvantage | A story complication hits that side: reinforcements, the floor gives way, an objective slips away. |
| Surge | Disadvantage | Wild magic or a terrain hazard flares on that side (fire spreads, a ward discharges). |
| Break | Villains only | Bloodied minions that miss an attack this round flee or surrender. |

## Deck composition (40 cards)

Shuffle a fresh 40-card deck at the start of each combat and draw without replacement until the fight ends, so good and bad streaks feel real. Use code to build, shuffle, and draw when it's available; otherwise track drawn card numbers in the status block. Each card has a Standard line, a Dramatic line, and a skill-resolution line.

| Line | Heroes first | Villains first | Effect mix per line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 26 cards | 14 cards | About 30% hero advantage, 20% villain disadvantage, 10% hero disadvantage, 10% villain advantage, 30% no effect |
| Dramatic | 14 cards | 26 cards | The mirror: villain advantages and hero disadvantages dominate |

Within each mix, weight the effects as Up 3, Inspiration 2, Flurry 1, Stymied 3, Confused 3, Fatigued 2, Setback 2, Surge 1. Break appears only as a villain effect on Standard lines.

## Dramatic Skill Resolution

Use this for chases, heists, rituals, and similar challenges. The adventure or the session lead sets the steps (A, B, C, D…) and the check for each.

- Steps must be completed in order, and a step can only be attempted on a round whose card shows its letter.
- **Possible Setback** on the card: a failed check that round loses one completed step.
- **Complication** on the card: a failed check that round makes every remaining step's DC 2 higher.
- Each card's skill line shows one or two letters, or one of these two results. Weight it roughly evenly across A–D, with about 1 in 5 cards showing Possible Setback or Complication.
