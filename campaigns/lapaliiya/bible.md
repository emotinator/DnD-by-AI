# Campaign Bible

_Last updated: 2026-10-19 · Sources: Nelgron's campaign event log, Entries 1–31 through "The Dawn Titans" (canon for what was played; `archive/chronicles/nelgron-campaign-event-log-entries-1-31.md`), and the previous DM's prep notes (`archive/prep/`). Prep is background only and is marked **[prep]** wherever play hasn't confirmed it. Where play and prep differ, play wins._

## The situation now

The party has just climbed out of the Underdark, at night, onto the slopes of the Adder Peaks before **the Burning Anvil**, the fire giant city of **Karrag-Dum**. They are exhausted and hurt. Below them, in the **Temple of the First Fire** beneath the Sunless Sea, **Kaitlin** (the decoy Caralyn) was offered the keeper's choice: hold the failing chains at the cost of her life, or save herself. The Rogue shouted for her to run, and she ran. **Two of the seven chains are broken. Five hold.**

The campaign has turned from a political kidnapping into something older: an ancient prison made of memory is failing, and the world is forgetting.

## The central mystery: the First Fire and the Seven Bindings

**What the party knows (from play):**
- Before the gods, "the First Fire burned without a keeper." Seven figures of different shapes and sizes bound immense elemental beings, "what could not be destroyed," in **seven places**, with chains of light spread through the earth.
- The bindings are made of memory: *"Seven memories became seven chains… what is bound and not remembered will one day be forgotten."*
- Altar readings at the temple: *"The First Fire cannot be commanded… cannot be fed with death alone. Let the broken be acknowledged. Let the bound be remembered. Let the keeper choose."* And: *"Speak what was lost. Remember what was bound. Give the Fire its name again."* The Fire's name is unknown. **[prep]** Per `archive/prep/prep-chat-a13-15-notes.md`, "giving the Fire its name" is a declaration of purpose, not a secret name: *"We remember those who came before. We remember those who were bound. We choose what we will carry forward."* The inscription's final words: *"A prison may endure beyond its makers. Only the living can decide whether it should."*
- Primordial floor runes: *"When the first fire faltered, seven hands held the world together."*
- The hanging tower was one of the seven stations. It fell when its chain broke during the fight with Zohir.
- Across the campaign, memory has been eroding: Mussum's forgetful monks, Shamph's blank archives, stripped memories, the lost three weeks, the nameless riders. This appears to be the bindings failing.
- The **seven lantern-bearers / Keepers**: six extinguished, one lit low (Mussum mural). The Mussum flame was carried to Davarn, where "One Keeper returns."

**DM-ONLY [prep]:**
- The imprisoned beings are the **Dawn Titans** (primordials). **Never label them evil.** The party has seen the prison, not the crime that justified it. Whether the imprisonment was just, and what the Titans would do if freed, stays open.
- The seven stations were built by a civilization older than any known people of Toril, as stabilizing anchors.
- **Spite** found the tower long after and built the corruption inside it; he didn't build the tower. Zohir found Spite's work and believed the binding could be controlled. She was wrong.
- **Kaitlin refused, so the prep's "Choice B" applies:** the temple could not reinforce the prison. The chains are loosening and the Titans' awakening has begun, though they are not free. **The next campaign phase runs on this consequence.**
- Theme line: *"The prisons were never the lock. Memory was the lock."* (A late reveal, not yet found.)
- Zohir's line *"It was built to contain what was already here"* is unexplained in prep. **DM-ONLY: undecided.**
- **Spite** himself: never named in play, and prep never says who or what he is. **DM-ONLY: undecided.** The Twisted Path prep gives his signature: whispers that turn party members against each other.

## Setting

- **Region:** the Vilhon Reach and the lands south (Forgotten Realms): Chondath, Tethyr, Lapaliiya, Amn.
- **Lapaliiya / Lapadiiya** (same place; spellings vary): the trade city where the party first met. The escort job began here.
- **Ormpetarr:** home base in Chondath, where the moon chant carried the party. **[prep]** Its rulers want the Amn connection handled quietly.
- **The Ember Quiet:** an Ilmateri order. Its monastery is where Nelgron trained; its Elder keeps a hospice for the dying, the "House That Bears."
- **The Chondalwood ("Chondalla Woods"):** a hostile, fey forest with shifting paths and drunk satyrs.
- **The Adder Peaks ("Mt. Aider"):** deep snow, avalanches, chained basalt-haulers on the volcano slope. **The basalt hollow:** a hidden bowl of hexagonal columns with a warm vent, ¼ mile west of where the party came out of the tube, out of sight of Karrag-Dum. A switchback hauling road runs below toward the city.
- **Karrag-Dum, "The Burning Anvil":** fire giant city with black basalt spires, a temple of Surtur, slave pens run by Azer, and an Amnian embassy. Map: `archive/maps/karrag-dum-city.png`. The party emerged outside it after the Dawn Titans; the log calls that identification unconfirmed, but prep and the Twisted Path overlook both point to it.
- **The party's lava tube:** a cooled tube into the closed market, used as a hidden base. The giants may not know it exists.
- **The Emerald Way:** the unwatched northern road to Davarn through millennia-dead cities.
- **Mussum:** an Ilmateri hospice whose monks forget their own days. Brother Malven keeps the last flame.
- **Shamph:** a dead city that feels watched. Roofless Temple of Tyr; archives worn blank; an unlit lantern inscribed *"Justice Remembers."* **[prep]** This is the extinguished **Second Vigil Flame**.
- **Maetherwatch / Maerthwatch:** a ruined watchtower with an ancient construct demanding a watchword. **[prep]** A former Vigil Post; its journal reads *"We do not guard the prison. We guard its memory."* The construct's last words: *"Then who remains to remember?"*
- **Davarn:** a thriving dwarven town of Moradin. The **Stone at Davarn**, a 100 ft standing stone, restores memory to miners who went too deep. **[prep]** The First Forge lies beneath; Moradin's first temple was built over something.
- **The Last Gate:** memorial doors below Davarn carved with thousands of names. *REMEMBER US / REMEMBER THOSE WHO WALKED BELOW / REMEMBER THOSE WHO DID NOT RETURN.*
- **The King's Highway:** a vast, featureless road predating the dwarves, marked with white Godstone that is part of the ward. Chain rings and dragged links along it.
- **The Myconid Garden:** a warm fissure of bioluminescent fungi.
- **The Twisted Path:** looping, shifting tunnels. **[prep]** Long rests in unprotected stretches give no benefit.
- **The Sunless Sea** (prep also says "Starless"): a black underground ocean under a crystal "sky." Cyclops pilgrims cross it.
- **The hanging tower ("the Stone in the Water"):** a ceiling-hung binding station. Collapsed into the sea.
- **The Temple of the First Fire:** a grown, organic temple behind a warm bronze door in a sunken city. Unexplored: a rune-inscribed construct blocking a passage, an endless shaft, a meditation area, a sack and coins. Partly collapsed after the choice.
- **[prep] Firebrand's Redoubt:** a fortress beyond Davarn where duergar dig, armed with the Obsidian Spike, no longer remembering why. Not reached in play.

## Factions

### Amn and the Council of Five
A merchant nation that outsources violence. Its embassy in Karrag-Dum is staffed by **the Mage** and a **soft-spoken, well-dressed Halfling**; its field agents wear **black and red**.
- **What happened:** Amn manipulated the fire giants into seizing the Hawklin bride to stop the **Tethyr–Lapaliiya alliance**. The giants took a decoy.
- **Public deal (treaty fragments):** the Council of Five recognizes an **"Ember Throne"** for the giants. Article Five: the individual is held until territorial guarantees are met.
- **Real plan (the letter to the Factor, in the party's hands):** the giants are "useful but predictably prideful"; delay recognition until excavation quotas are met; keep the forge clans feuding; deny the Embercrown line coastal access; replacement claimants are ready "if the queen proves difficult."
- **[prep]** Some Amnian consortium leaders may now want everyone involved dead. The decoy is a loose end.
- **DM-ONLY [prep]:** Amnian excavation weakened the seals under Ormpetarr. What Amn is digging for in the Adder Peaks may be a binding. **DM-ONLY: undecided.**
- **Factor Valance:** the coin's destination (see Saressa). Possibly the Factor of the letter. Unconfirmed.

### The Cowled Wizards (Amn's licensed mages)
- **Saressa**, a Cowled Wizard, speaks through a coin the party carries. She warned them to stay on the path and keep to the white stones. The coin is to go to Factor Valance on her behalf.
- **[prep]** The prep's patron "**Seressa**" fits Saressa's role: she has been below before, is genuinely worried, and doesn't know everything. She offered maps, political cover, limited magic, and the truth that the giants were manipulated. **Resolved 2026-10-28:** the previous DM's prep chat has the coin's voice as Seressa, so prep-Seressa and Saressa are the same person (name drift). **DM-ONLY [prep]:** she isn't fully honest. She knows the Tower-type structures as "anchors" from her own research into magical disturbances beneath Karrag-Dum, and she is tempted by what they can do. See `archive/prep/prep-chat-a13-15-notes.md`. (Not the party sorceress Seressa, and not Nelgron's old alias Seressa Vale.)

### The fire giants of Karrag-Dum
- **Ruled by a queen of the Embercrown line** (from the Factor letter). **[prep]** Suggested name: **Queen Hrothgarda Embercrown**; confirm.
- The forge clans are rivals. In Kiln Hall a Huge giant smashed a smaller fire giant mid-fight.
- **[prep]** Shown the letter: *"You would have me believe I am being forged… into a tool?"*
- The ember feast: the transfer deadline in the ambushers' note. Moot now that the decoy is known, unless the giants still hold something.

### The Ember Quiet
An Ilmateri order. Its Elder runs a hospice for the dying and gave Nelgron menial work, not teaching. Nelgron belongs to it.

### Cults of the dark
- **Loviatar:** a cult fed on the hospice's patients, then attacked it at night. Driven off.
- **Shar:** **[prep]** defiled Selûnite sites (the Temple Below the Snow was a Selûnite temple; the gnoll priestess's ritual at the chasm was Sharran). Prep ending option: if not fully stopped, "shadows spill into the Shaar later."
- **[prep]** Sister Vaelis and the Lantern Below (see Ormpetarr side arc, below).

### The cyclops pilgrims
Three cyclopes ferry pilgrims across the Sunless Sea, once mislabelled "ogres." They said the hanging structures "weren't there a fortnight ago" and the chains have grown louder. They gave every party member an identical gem-stone, and one said of Kaitlin: *"I sense this may be needed."* They know more than they say. **[prep]** Leader: **Ogrun Stone-Eye** (not named in play).
