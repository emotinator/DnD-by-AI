\# Session 11 – The King’s Highway Target: Level 4 characters Tone: Claustrophobic horror → cosmic unease → alien wonder Goal: Reach the Myconid Garden and learn the first truth about the prison.

\---

\# Opening (10 minutes)

\#\#\# Recap text (Discord or read aloud)

**\> The road beneath Davarn descends through ancient dwarven workings, but the stone has gradually changed. The chisel marks vanished miles ago. The walls became smooth, fused, and impossibly hard. \> \> Somewhere ahead lies the route toward Firebrand’s Redoubt and the deeper caverns beyond. \> \> The dwarves call it The Last Gate. \> \> None of them willingly travel it after dark.**

# **Session 11 Opening: The Sealed Gate**

The dwarves do not call it the King's Highway.

They call it...

> **The Last Gate.**

This is the final worked stone of Davarn.

The final place where Moradin's children still claim dominion.

Beyond...

is the Deep.

---

## **Read Aloud**

> **The descending tunnel ends before an immense pair of stone doors, their surfaces blackened by age yet unmarred by time.**

> **Every inch is carved with dwarven runes—not boasts of kings or victories in battle, but names.**

> **Thousands of names.**

> **Each painstakingly chiseled into the stone.**

> **Between them are simple prayers.**

> ***Remember us.***

> ***Remember those who walked below.***

> ***Remember those who did not return.***

---

Standing before the doors is an elderly dwarf.

Not a warrior.

Not a priest.

Simply...

a Keeper.

His only duty is opening and closing the gate.

---

He asks one question.

> "Do you truly wish to pass beyond the Last Gate?"

Not dramatic.

Matter-of-fact.

---

If the players agree...

he shakes his head sadly.

> "Then the Deep knows your names."

---

# **The Ritual**

This is where I think we formalize the separation of worlds.

The Keeper explains:

> "No dwarf opens these doors without the Rite of Return."

---

Each character is invited to place one hand on the stone.

The Keeper asks each of them one question.

Not mechanical.

Personal.

---

Examples:

To Valerian:

> "What would you refuse to forget?"

To Melania:

> "What truth would you suffer to preserve?"

To Theon:

> "Whom do you still honor?"

To Bubba:

> "What path always leads you home?"

To Huretha

	“Who are your people?”

To Arinir

	“Why must you carry the light?”

To Nilarion

	“What is the path you follow now?”

(The absent PCs can answer these next session.)

---

The Keeper carves each answer—not the words, but a single rune—into a small slate.

He keeps them.

---

Then he says:

> "If you return unable to answer your own question...

> ...we will know the Deep has begun to take you."

No one explains further.

---

# **The Skill Challenge**

Instead of "roll Survival."

Let's make it ceremonial.

**Complexity:** 8 successes before 3 failures.

Everyone participates.

Possible skills:

**History**  
 Recognize ancient dwarf wards.

**Religion**  
 Offer proper respect.

**Insight**  
 Understand the Keeper's warnings.

**Survival**  
 Prepare equipment.

**Medicine**  
 Pack against cave sickness.

**Nature**  
 Recognize dangerous fungi.

**Arcana**  
 Feel magical pressure beyond the gate.

**Athletics**  
 Help turn the ancient mechanisms.

---

Each success contributes to opening the gate.

Not because the rolls physically matter.

Because entering the Deep is something **everyone** must commit to.

---

# **Opening the Doors**

No dramatic grinding.

Instead...

The Keeper inserts a stone key.

The runes begin glowing.

One by one...

the carved names illuminate.

Thousands of lights.

Like stars.

Then...

every light goes dark.

The gate opens.

---

Behind it...

absolute blackness.

No air.

No sound.

Even darkvision struggles.

---

The Keeper whispers:

> "Moradin forged the gate.

> We merely keep it closed."

---

Then he bars the doors behind them.

\# Act I – The Descent (60–75 minutes)

\#\# Scene 1: The Last Dwarven Marker

A final carved warning:

\> “BEYOND THIS POINT THE ROAD IS NOT OURS.”

**The tunnel widens unnaturally.**

**Fifty feet across.**

**Thirty feet high.**

**Perfectly straight.**

**No support pillars.**

**No tool marks.**

The players should immediately feel:

\> "This was not built by dwarves."

\---

\#\# Environmental Horror

\#\#\# The Stone

**\- Black with veins of pale white crystal. \- Warm in some places, freezing in others. \- Sound travels strangely. \- Their footsteps echo twice.**

\#\#\# The Chains

Every few hundred feet they find:

**\- massive iron rings embedded in the walls \- chain links the size of wagon wheels \- some snapped \- some dragged across the floor, leaving grooves in the stone**

No explanation.

\---

\#\# First Unease

Ask for passive Perception.

Whoever notices hears:

\> drag... \> \> drag... \> \> drag...

Far away.

Then silence.

Never explain it.

\---

\# Scene 2: The Bloodstone Chamber

This is your first "we shouldn't be here" moment.

The highway passes through a circular chamber.

The floor is covered in dark red crystalline growths.

Not gems.

They pulse faintly.

The air smells like copper and wet stone.

\#\#\# Discovery

Touching a crystal causes a brief vision:

\- a colossal hand chained to a mountain \- molten blood flowing through stone \- a voice that is not a language

No damage.

Just dread.

\#\#\# Mechanical Effect

For the next hour:

\- advantage on detecting hidden creatures \- disadvantage on saves against fear

The stone is awakening their instincts.

\---

\# First Combat – Carrion Crawler Ambush (20–25 minutes)

Use 2 Carrion Crawlers \+ 4 Tomb Worms (from Tome of Beasts).

\#\#\# Why Tomb Worms?

They fit perfectly: \- small corpse-feeding worms \- emerge from cracks in the bloodstone \- create that skin-crawling horror

\#\#\# Tactics

The crawlers attack from the ceiling.

The worms erupt from the floor around paralyzed characters.

Describe:

**\- slime dripping onto armor \- antennae brushing faces \- worms wriggling through cracks in gauntlets**

This should feel gross rather than heroic.

\---

\# Act II – The Highway That Should Not Exist (75–90 minutes)

After the fight, the tunnel opens onto the true King’s Highway.

\#\# Read Aloud

**\> The tunnel ends abruptly. \> \> Before you stretches a road so vast it could carry ten wagons abreast. It vanishes into darkness in both directions, perfectly straight, as though drawn across the earth by a god. \> \> Along its center run blocks of pale stone that glow faintly beneath the dust. \> \> The ceiling is lost in shadow. \> \> Somewhere in the distance, you hear chains moving through the dark.**

\---

\#\# The Godstone

The pale blocks are Godstone.

\#\#\# Effects

\- divine magic feels unusually clear \- detect magic reveals overwhelming abjuration \- anyone touching it feels a momentary sense of safety

Then:

\> "The safety is not for you. It is for what lies below."

\---

\#\# The Seressa Coin

Perfect opportunity.

The coin suddenly becomes warm.

Seressa’s voice comes through faintly, distorted.

**\> "If you can hear this, you have reached the white stone. Listen carefully: do not leave the road unless you must. The darkness between the stones is... hungry."**

Then the connection fails.

This tells the players: \- she has been here \- she is genuinely concerned \- she does not know everything

\---

\# Scene 3: The Abandoned Encampment

A dwarven expedition from decades ago.

\- rusted tools \- bedrolls \- a journal

\#\#\# The Journal

The writing deteriorates.

Last entries:

**\> "The chains move when no one watches." \> \> "Borin says he hears someone digging beneath the road." \> \> "We found footprints larger than a giant’s." \> \> "The road is getting wider."**

**The final page is blank except for one sentence scratched repeatedly:**

**\> "DO NOT LOOK INTO THE CRACKS."**

\---

\# Second Combat – Hook Horror Stalkers (25–30 minutes)

Use 2 Hook Horrors \+ 1 Cave Fisher.

\#\#\# Horror Twist

The hook horrors do not attack immediately.

For several minutes: \- scraping sounds above \- silhouettes crossing the ceiling \- stones falling nearby

Let the players become paranoid.

Then the Cave Fisher hooks someone and drags them toward a side crevasse while the hook horrors descend.

This becomes a tense rescue rather than a straightforward fight.

\# Act III – The Gardeners (60–75 minutes)

The highway eventually reaches a vast fissure.

Warm, humid air rises from below.

Bioluminescent fungi glow in soft blues and greens.

For the first time all session, the darkness is not oppressive.

It is beautiful.

And utterly alien.

\---

\#\# The Myconid Garden

Describe:

\- towering mushrooms like cathedral pillars \- spores drifting like snow \- silent figures tending fungal growths \- pale vines growing through cracks in the Godstone

The Myconids are not waiting for them.

They are working.

Gardening.

Repairing.

\---

\#\# The Alien Encounter

No Common.

No Undercommon.

The Myconids release spores.

Each character experiences a shared vision.

\#\#\# The Vision

\- the highway glowing with fresh Godstone \- enormous chains being lowered into the earth \- seven figures carrying torches \- one torch extinguishing \- something vast moving beneath the stone

Then the vision ends.

The Myconids simply return to their work.

\---

\#\# The Gardener’s Gift

One elder Myconid approaches and places a small glowing fungus in the party’s hands.

Through spores they understand a single concept:

\> "Wound. Heal. Deep below."

The fungus functions as: \- 1/day lesser restoration \- glows brighter near corrupted stone

\---

\# Session End (10 minutes)

As the party rests near the garden:

\- the spores create a peaceful atmosphere \- for the first time all session they feel safe

Then, just before sleep:

\> drag... \> \> drag... \> \> drag...

Much closer now.

A chain scrapes across stone somewhere beyond the darkness.

The Myconids do not react.

Which is far more terrifying than if they did.

Fade to black.

\---

\# Tome of Beasts Additions

I’d absolutely use these:

\#\# Excellent Fits

\#\#\# Tomb Worms Corpse-feeding horror.

\#\#\# Cave Fisher Perfect for vertical terror.

\#\#\# Bone Swarm A failed expedition reduced to animated fragments.

\#\#\# Shadow Mastiff If you want a hunting presence.

\#\#\# Nullifier If you want one truly terrifying magical anomaly later in the arc.

\---

\# What the Players Learn

By the end of Session 11 they should know:

\- The King’s Highway predates dwarves. \- The white stone is part of an enormous ward. \- Something colossal was chained here. \- Seressa has investigated this before. \- The Myconids are not enemies. \- The Underdark is trying to heal a wound. \- Whatever moves in the darkness is still moving.
