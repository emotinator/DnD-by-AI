# **D\&D Finale: The Dawn Titans**

Adventure 14–15 combined · 7 characters · Level 4 · 5–6 hours

Adventure premise: Beneath the Sunless Sea, the party discovers an ancient temple that predates the known civilizations of Toril. Here, the characters learn that seven ancient binding stations hold the Dawn Titans prisoner—and that the prison is failing. Caitlyn may be able to strengthen it, but only by sacrificing herself. The party must decide whether to allow her sacrifice or prevent it and risk the Titans beginning to awaken.

Whichever choice they make, the temple begins to collapse. The characters must race up an ancient lava tube and emerge on the slopes outside Karrag-Dum, the Burning Anvil, the fire giant city. The adventure ends there, with the decision's consequences unresolved.

The guiding principles are that Caitlyn is a person with agency, not a mechanism to be activated, and the Dawn Titans remain mysterious. The party learns what the ancient prison does, but not whether its captives are evil, misunderstood, or something beyond those categories.

# **Act I — Across the Sunless Sea**

## **Encounter 1: The Cyclops Pilgrims**

![Free AI Art Generator: Create AI Art from Text - SeaArt AI][image2]

The party meets Ogrun Stone-Eye and two fellow pilgrims aboard an enormous wooden boat. They ferry the characters across the glowing underground sea but refuse to approach the distant Temple of Primordial Fire.

Ogrun will take them to the shore of a partially submerged city built from enormous basalt blocks and bronze fittings. Its scale foreshadows fire giant architecture, although the ruins predate Karrag-Dum by thousands of years.

> "We cross these waters," Ogrun says. "We do not go there."

He gives the party a small stone token for safe passage back to the shoreline. He knows the temple is ancient, but not what it contains.

## **Encounter 2: The Drowned City**

The party explores a flooded plaza, a collapsed giant foundry, and a sealed chamber. Use one short combat against aquatic or subterranean creatures disturbed by the party's arrival; for seven level-4 characters, make the battlefield interesting with submerged passages, unstable ledges, and rising water rather than simply adding lots of enemies.

Inside the foundry, the characters discover a relief depicting seven stations connected by chains. One station is broken. An inscription, partially worn away, reads:

> "When the First Fire faltered, seven hands held the world together."

The party can also find 600–800 gp worth of ancient coins, bronze ingots, and luminous crystals, plus two magic items: a Cloak of the Manta Ray and a Lantern of Revealing.

## **The ascent platform**

At the far end of the ruins, an ancient circular platform floats above the sea. It is damaged, flexible, and alarmingly wobbly.

Run a short skill challenge: six successes before three failures. Athletics, Acrobatics, Arcana, Investigation, and creative spell use can all help. Failures cause falls across the platform, temporary gravity reversals, or chunks of stone breaking away.

When the mechanism activates, gravity turns sideways. The characters fall upward toward the cavern ceiling, with the Sunless Sea dropping away beneath them. Seven immense chains emerge from the temple and disappear into the rock overhead.

One chain is broken.

Another glows faintly.

The party reaches the temple.

# **Act II — The Temple of Primordial Fire**

**![この題材ではSD1.5 が FLUX.1 より圧倒的な魅力。そんなこともあるのか。｜EnigMind][image3]**  
**The Temple Is Alive**

**The temple does not look built. It looks grown.**

**Its walls curve like the inside of an enormous shell. Passages narrow and widen organically. Pale stone veins glow with amber light. There are no familiar gods, no statues, and no right angles.**

The atmosphere is no longer horror. It is awe—but something about the place feels profoundly wrong.

### 

### **Encounter 3: The Memory Gallery**

The characters enter a chamber where the walls contain memories instead of inscriptions.

Touching a wall reveals a brief vision: seven figures of different shapes and sizes standing around seven immense elemental beings. Chains of light spread through the earth. The figures bind the beings, and the vision ends before revealing why.

A second memory shows the seven stations. One of them is the Tower the party recently escaped.

The characters learn three things:

* The Tower was one of seven binding stations.  
* The stations were built to maintain a prison holding the Dawn Titans.  
* The prison is failing, and the Temple is struggling to compensate.

They do not learn whether the Dawn Titans are evil, whether the original imprisonment was justified, or what the Titans would do if freed.

### **Encounter 4: The Temple's Defenders**

![Ah Puch and the Gods of the Underworld: A Global Perspective - Mythology WorldWide][image4]

At the inner threshold, ancient guardians awaken—not out of malice, but because the temple detects a failing binding.

For seven level-4 characters, use a moderate encounter with a suitable construct or elemental guardian and two lesser defenders. Add one environmental complication each round: a floor section tilts, a pulse of heat crosses the chamber, or gravity briefly shifts.

The guardians cease fighting if the party restores a damaged control symbol or otherwise demonstrates that they are trying to stabilize the mechanism. Combat is a possible solution, not the only one. Beyond them lies the First Fire.

## **The Heart of the Temple**

**The party enters an enormous chamber. Seven chains stretch into darkness. At its centre floats a sphere of elemental energy: fire, molten stone, lightning, ash, and something resembling starlight, all existing together.**

**Seven colossal silhouettes lie beyond the chains.**

**Caitlyn steps forward. The temple's light gathers around her.**

**She feels the mechanism recognizes her—not as a princess, but as a possible living keeper.**

The temple reveals the terrible bargain: Caitlyn can enter the First Fire and reinforce the prison. Her life will be consumed in the process. The prison will hold more securely, though no one can promise for how long.

Without that sacrifice, the weakened binding will continue to fail.

Caitlyn can explain what she understands, but she does not know why the temple has chosen her. She has not been secretly destined for this role; she is simply the person the failing mechanism has identified as a viable sacrifice.

# **Act III — Caitlyn's Choice**

Give the players time to talk to Caitlyn, search for alternatives, examine the mechanism, and attempt magic. Reward clever ideas, but don't make the dilemma disappear through one lucky skill check.

Caitlyn should have her own view. She is frightened, angry that others have repeatedly decided her worth for her, and unwilling to treat her own life as insignificant.

> **"All this time, everyone else has been deciding what I am worth."**

> **She looks at the chains.**

> **"A princess. A bargaining chip. A prisoner. A key."**

> **She turns to you.**

> **"I don't want to be a key. But if I can save everyone... I need to know what you think."**

> **The First Fire brightens.**

> **"What do we do?"**

The party must choose.

## **Choice A — Allow the sacrifice**

Caitlyn enters the First Fire willingly. The seven chains flare with light, and the prison strengthens. Caitlyn dies, and the party has chosen to honour her willingness to save the world at immense personal cost.

The Dawn Titans remain imprisoned. The party does not know whether the prison will hold forever.

## **Choice B — Prevent the sacrifice**

The characters pull Caitlyn away, disrupt the mechanism, or find another way to stop the ritual. Caitlyn survives, but the Temple cannot reinforce the prison.

The chains begin to loosen. The silhouettes stir. The Dawn Titans are not yet free, but their awakening has begun.

Neither outcome is a failure. Don't tell the players which is right, and don't have Caitlyn make the decision for them. Let their choice matter.

# **Act IV — Escape Through the Lava Tube**

Immediately after the choice, the temple begins to collapse. The First Fire surges, the gravity-transfer mechanism fails, and the chamber splits open. A steep volcanic tube is revealed behind the inner sanctuary—a natural conduit leading toward the surface of the Adder Peaks.

The party must climb.

### **The escape challenge**

Run this as a fast-paced skill challenge: five successes before three failures. Each player should contribute something different.

Possible obstacles:

* A ledge breaks away beneath the party.  
* A blast of superheated steam fills the passage.  
* Gravity reverses for a few terrifying seconds.  
* A stream of molten rock blocks the way.  
* A chain whips through the tube as the binding machinery tears apart.  
* One character slips and must be caught by another.

Use Athletics, Acrobatics, Survival, Perception, appropriate spells, and creative teamwork. On a failure, deal modest damage or separate the party briefly; the objective is to create urgency, not to kill exhausted characters after the major choice.

**As they climb, the characters hear the chains below.**

**CLANK.**

**The tube shakes.**

**CLANK.**

The firelight behind them changes. Whether Caitlyn sacrificed herself or survived, the temple is disappearing into darkness.

At the final bend, the passage opens.

### **Read aloud**

> **You scramble over the final ridge of black volcanic stone.**

> **Heat washes over you—not the unnatural heat of the temple, but the living warmth of a mountain.**

> **You emerge into the open air.**

> **Above you, the Adder Peaks rise into the darkness.**

> **Before you stands an immense fortress of black stone, its towers glowing from within, its chimneys pouring smoke into the night.**

> **The Burning Anvil.**

> **Karrag-Dum.**

> **You have escaped the world beneath the world.**

> **But far below, something enormous has moved.**

> **And somewhere beneath the mountain, a chain rings once more.**

End the session here.

No final fight. No explanation from Seressa. No confirmation of whether the Dawn Titans will break free. Let the characters—and players—sit with the choice they made.

## **Dungeon Master notes**

* Combat balance: For seven level-4 characters, use moderate encounters and environmental mechanics. Avoid stacking several high-damage enemies in the temple; the climax should be the decision, not a character death caused by excessive combat difficulty.  
* The Dawn Titans: Never label them evil. The party has witnessed an ancient prison, not the crime that justified it.  
* Caitlyn: Give her agency, relationships, fear, and hope. If she dies, her sacrifice is her own choice—but the party decides whether to allow it.  
* The next campaign: Either ending creates a compelling future. If Caitlyn dies, the party must live with the cost of preserving the prison. If she survives, they must confront an awakening that may threaten the world—or reveal that the ancient history they trusted was incomplete.
