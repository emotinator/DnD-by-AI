# **The Shattered Sanctum**

*Former Selûnite Temple, now defiled by Shar*

## **Dungeon Tone**

* **Light vs Darkness**: Moonlight is a literal mechanic—areas touched by moonlight weaken creatures of darkness.

* **Wind & Sand**: Low howling wind, drifting sand, and half-buried reliefs.

* **Profaned Sacred Space**: Selûnite symbols cracked or inverted; Shar’s iconography carved over rather than replacing—very petty, very Shar.

---

## **Room 1 – The Fallen Dome (Entrance)**

**Visuals:**  
 *A once-grand dome lies collapsed inward. Moonlight spills through a jagged opening, illuminating a broken statue of Selûne—her face intact, arms shattered.*

**Features:**

* Selûnite star motifs half-buried in sand.

* Moonlight creates **dim light** in the center.

**Encounter – Shadow-Scavengers**

* **2 Shadows** (MM) *OR* reskin as “Moon-Husked Shades”

* **3 Gnoll Witherlings** (reskinned gnolls warped by Shar)

**Tactical Twist:**  
 While standing in direct moonlight, **Shadows have disadvantage on Stealth checks** and cannot hide.

**Clue:**  
 A shattered altar bears claw marks and a carved Sharran spiral.

---

## **Room 2 – Hall of Phases (Trap \#1)**

**Visuals:**  
 *A long hall with moon-phase mosaics along the floor—new moon to full moon.*

**Trap – The False Eclipse**

* **Trigger:** Stepping on the *new moon* tile.

* **Effect:**

  * DC 12 Dex save or fall into a **10-ft sand-filled pit**

  * Restrained until an action is used to escape (DC 11 Str/Athletics)

**Hint:**  
 The *full moon* tiles are slightly raised and dust-free.

**Lore:**  
 This hall once taught Selûnite novices the sacred calendar.

---

## **Room 3 – The Moon Pool (Desecrated Font)**

**Visuals:**  
 *A cracked circular basin once filled with blessed water. Now stagnant and blackened, reflecting no light.*

**Encounter – Corrupted Guardians**

* **1 Darkmantle** (reskinned as a “Fallen Moon Ray”)

* **2 Shadow Mastiffs (Pups)**  
   *(Use Dire Wolf stats, reduce HP slightly)*

**Environmental Feature:**  
 If the party cleanses the pool (holy water, moonlight \+ prayer, etc.):

* Once per dungeon, a PC may gain **advantage on a save vs. fear or darkness**.

---

## **Room 4 – Reliquary of Silver Tears**

**Visuals:**  
 *Stone niches once held relics—now shattered. A silver crescent pendant lies half-buried in sand.*

**Roleplay / Exploration Room**

* Pendant of Selûne (non-magical but sacred).

* Murals show Selûne shielding travelers from darkness.

**Optional Encounter (if noisy):**

* **3 Starved Gnolls** arguing over loot (easy combat or intimidation).

**Reward:**

* The pendant glows faintly in moonlight, acting as a **compass toward the final chamber**.

---

## **Room 5 – Sacrificial Chapel (Trap \#2)**

**Visuals:**  
 *Black candles, dried blood, and a cracked altar with Shar’s symbol carved over Selûne’s.*

**Trap – Whispering Darkness**

* **Trigger:** Touching the altar.

* **Effect:**

  * DC 12 Wis save or suffer **disadvantage on next attack or save**

  * Victim hears Shar whisper doubts and secrets

**Hidden Feature:**  
 Under the altar is a sealed Selûnite prayer-scroll warning of “the eclipse below.”

---

## **Room 6 – Cells of the Faithful**

**Visuals:**  
 *Small stone cells—some collapsed, some sealed. Scratches on the walls: prayers etched in desperation.*

**Encounter – The Lost**

* **1 Shadow** (former priest)

* **2 Cultists of Shar** (MM Cultist stats)

**Roleplay Option:**  
 If the Shadow is spoken to in Selûnite prayers, it hesitates for one round.

**Clue:**  
 Mentions of a **midnight ritual** performed “beneath the moon’s wound.”

---

## **Room 7 – The Lunar Wound (Final Chamber)**

**Visuals:**  
 *The sanctum’s heart. The roof is entirely gone—moonlight pours in. A massive crack splits the floor, descending into darkness.*

**Boss Encounter – Sharran Ritual**

* **1 Sharran Acolyte** (Acolyte stats \+ Darkness once/day)

* **1 Shadow**

* **2 Gnoll Fanatics** (use Gnoll stats, \+1 HP)

**Dynamic Terrain:**

* Areas in moonlight grant **\+1 to saving throws**.

* Areas of magical darkness impose **disadvantage on Perception**.

**Ending Options:**

* Disrupt the ritual → the crack seals partially.

* Complete desecration → shadows spill into the Shaar later (future hook).

---

## **Treasure & Aftermath**

* Silvered dagger (50 gp)

* Selûnite scrolls (religion lore, advantage on future Selûne checks)
