## Seressa's coin advice at the Tower (Adventure 13, Location 1: the Seven Symbols)
- The coin warms. *"I hear you. Where are you?"* On hearing about the seven symbols: *"Don't touch anything. ... Actually... touch nothing."*
- She's a wizard trying hard not to sound excited. She knows the Tower is ancient and dangerous, but not the Dawn Titan story.
- **What she says:**
  1. The symbols are "functional. Old magic. Very old. Older than the dwarves who built Karrag-Dum." How old? *"I don't know. And that is precisely what concerns me."*
  2. *"Do not experiment with the glowing one. If the others are dormant, there is a reason."*
  3. On the memories: *"A memory can be genuine and still be incomplete. And an incomplete truth can be more dangerous than a lie."*
  4. *"Then this isn't a temple. It's a mechanism."* For what? *"I don't know... Yet."*
  5. On Zohir Totek: *"He believes understanding a thing means possessing it. Don't make the same mistake."*
  6. She recognizes one symbol from old texts: such structures *"aren't called towers. They're called... anchors."*
  7. Parting words: *"You have found something that was deliberately hidden... I strongly recommend that you don't break it. Unless, of course, you have a very good reason."*
- **DM-ONLY secret:** Seressa isn't fully honest. She knows the word "anchor" from her own research into the magical disturbances beneath Karrag-Dum. She doesn't know about the Dawn Titans, or that there are seven. This is the moment she realizes the phenomenon under Karrag-Dum is tied to an ancient network, and she is **tempted**: *"I need to know what this thing can do."* Keep her ambiguous.
- **Name note:** the prep consistently has the coin's voice as **Seressa**. The table log says **Saressa**. Treat them as the same person (the table's spellings drift on purpose).

## Healing the altar (the inscription at the heart of the Temple)
- The inscription is carved in seven concentric rings around the broken altar. The outer rings are older than dwarvish; the innermost ring changes as it is read. The full text is in `bible.md`. It adds the opening lines: *"Before the gods named the heavens, before the mountains learned to stand, the First Fire burned without a keeper."*
- **Three steps to heal it:**
  1. **Acknowledge the broken:** repair a cracked rune, restore a fallen stone, or reconnect a severed channel. The altar is damaged, not evil.
  2. **Remember the bound:** speak aloud what they've learned. The altar responds to truth, not a password.
  3. **Give the Fire its name again:** not a secret name, but a declaration of purpose:
     > "We remember those who came before. / We remember those who were bound. / We choose what we will carry forward."
     The altar then lights up, briefly showing seven immense silhouettes.
- **Final words**, in a language no PC recognizes. Seressa could later identify it as older than any giant, dwarf or elven script:
  > "A prison may endure beyond its makers. / Only the living can decide whether it should."
- Caitlyn could become the altar's living keeper, but the inscription doesn't say she must.
