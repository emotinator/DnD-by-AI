**The Lanterns Beneath Moonfall Court**

A Forgotten Realms Adventure Module for 4 Level-2 Characters

# **Adventure Summary**

Following the rescue of Lady Caralyn Hawklin from the fire giant city of Karrag-Dum, the heroes return to Ormpetarr seeking rest. Instead, strange disappearances and failing moon-lanterns draw them into the flooded cisterns beneath Moonfall Court. Ancient shadows stir below the city, while political tensions quietly rise above.

# **Opening Scene: Seressa's Proposal**

Read Aloud:

 The rain falls softly across the slate rooftops of Ormpetarr as a cloaked figure waits beneath the shattered archways of the Moonfall Court gardens. Silver lantern light flickers against wet stone. A young woman in dark blue robes steps forward, calm and composed despite the late hour.

 “You have become inconvenient people,” she says quietly. “Which means you have also become important.”

 Her eyes briefly scan each of you before settling on Lady Caralyn.

 “There are deeper things moving beneath these mountains than treaties and kingdoms. If you wish to survive what comes next, we may eventually need one another.”

Her proposal is essentially:

Usually they merely disturb older things.”

# **Act I: The Temple of Selûne**

Read Aloud:

 Moonlight spills through the broken dome of the temple, casting pale silver reflections across cracked marble floors. Priests move quietly between rows of candles, their voices hushed and anxious.

 An elderly priestess approaches.

 “Three citizens vanished this week beneath Moonfall Court,” she says. “They returned changed. Sleepwalking. Whispering about a lantern below the city.”

 She lowers her voice.

 “We believe something beneath Ormpetarr has awakened.”

# **🌙 Core Premise**

Following the return of “Princess” Caralyn, strange events begin occurring near the Selûnite district:

* people sleepwalk at night,  
* moon lanterns extinguish themselves,  
* citizens disappear briefly and return confused,  
* and pale figures are seen beneath abandoned cistern tunnels.

The Temple of Selûne quietly asks the four heroes for help because:

* the city guard is compromised politically,  
* panic must be avoided,  
* and several priests suspect Sharite involvement.

But the truth is stranger.

Atmosphere:

# **Encounter: Lantern Wretches**

Three Lantern Wretches attack from the flooded maintenance halls. These former workers glow faintly from within, their ribcages illuminated by dying moon-lantern fire.

 Tactics:  
 \- They attempt to drag characters into deeper water.  
 \- Bright radiant light weakens them.  
 \- Reflections in the water create confusion and false movement.

* Ghoul stats with lower HP,  
* necrotic-flavored attacks.

# **🕯️ PUZZLE OPTION 1 — The Lunar Cycle Braziers**

*(Strongest Core Puzzle)*

## **Concept**

Three braziers represent:

* New Moon  
* Half Moon  
* Full Moon

The players must light them:

## **in the proper cycle.**

But Sharite vandals intentionally altered the symbols.

---

## **Visual Clues**

Selûnite imagery survives beneath damage:

* tides,  
* wolves,  
* silver flowers,  
* night birds.

A successful:

* Religion,  
* Nature,  
* or Insight check  
   reveals:  
   Selûne represents:

> change through cycles, not static perfection.

---

## **Correct Order**

### **New Moon**

(dim light)

### **Half Moon**

(growing light)

### **Full Moon**

(brilliant illumination)

When lit correctly:

* silver light spreads across flooded water,  
* hidden runes appear,  
* sealed doors unlock.

---

## **Failure Effect**

Incorrect order:

* shadows briefly animate,  
* whispers fill the room,  
* water darkens.

After 2 failures:

## **2 minor Shadows emerge.**

Not deadly.  
 Just pressure.

# **🌑 PUZZLE OPTION 2 — Cleansing the Graffiti**

*(Great roleplay layer)*

Examples:

> “Forget and be free.”

Players initially may try scraping them away physically.

But:  
 the corruption only fades if:

## **Selûnite prayers or acts of memory are spoken aloud.**

Sister Vaelis attempts to awaken the Lantern Below, a sealed shadow-consciousness hidden beneath Ormpetarr for centuries.
