# **THE TWISTED PATH**

### **Adventure 12 — Into the Deep**

**For 7 characters of approximately 4th level**  
**Estimated play time:** 4–5 hours  
**Setting:** The Underdark, beyond the King's Highway  
**Tone:** Mystery • Wonder • Psychological Horror • Reality Distortion

---

## **ADVENTURE SUMMARY**

The characters leave the strange sanctuary of the Myconids and continue deeper into the Underdark. They soon discover that the passages ahead do not obey the normal laws of geography—or perhaps even reality.

Something has begun to **twist the natural world**.

Spite's influence is reaching farther than anyone realized. The characters experience strange alterations to objects, spells, memories, and even their own impulses. They encounter creatures that are adapting to this influence rather than serving it.

The Myconids recognize the changes but do not consider them evil. To them, this is simply **nature responding to something new**.

The characters eventually encounter a mysterious mind flayer whose memories are fragmented and who seems to know more about the deep places than he should. After a dangerous confrontation with a creature that transforms from an ooze into a gibbering, memory-consuming horror, the characters reach a vast overlook.

There they see:

The Starless Sea, something enormous stirs in chains beneath.

---

# **ADVENTURE HOOK**

The characters have survived the horrors of the King's Highway and passed through the Myconid Garden.

Their route forward lies somewhere beyond the fungal caverns.

They have no reliable map.

The Underdark seems to have swallowed the boundaries between known places.

The characters' immediate goal is simple:

> **Find a safe route toward the dwarves they are looking for.**

But the deeper they travel, the less certain they become that the route is actually taking them where they intend.

---

# **ADVENTURE THEMES**

Throughout the adventure, emphasize three ideas.

### **MEMORY**

What has happened before?  
Who remembers it?  
Can memories be trusted?

### **ADAPTATION**

The creatures and environment are changing.

Not because they are evil.

Because they are **responding**.

### **SPITE**

Something is attempting to turn the characters against one another.

It does not need to defeat them.

It only needs them to destroy themselves.

---

# **THE TWISTED PATH**

## **Special Rule: Reality Is Unstable**

For this adventure only, reality occasionally behaves incorrectly.

The Dungeon Master can trigger a **Reality Twist** whenever the characters enter a significant new location, cast particularly powerful magic, or when dramatic tension needs to rise.

Roll 1d6 or choose an effect.

| d6 | Reality Twist |
| ----- | ----- |
| 1 | **Spell Wrongness:** A spell works normally, but its visual effect is bizarre or disturbing. |
| 2 | **Object Memory:** An object briefly appears as it looked decades or centuries ago. |
| 3 | **Broken Distance:** Something appears much closer or farther away than it actually is. |
| 4 | **Impossible Reflection:** A character sees something different in a reflection, shadow, or pool of water. |
| 5 | **Memory Echo:** A character hears a sentence spoken earlier in the adventure. Nobody else hears it. |
| 6 | **Spite's Whisper:** A character hears a voice encouraging them to distrust or attack another character. |

### **Important**

These effects should **not feel like arbitrary punishment**.

Reality is becoming strange.

The players should gradually realize:

> **Something is wrong with the world itself.**

# **PART ONE \- THE PATH THAT WASN'T THERE**

### **Read-Aloud**

> **The fungal gardens gradually disappear behind you. The warm air remains, but the great mushrooms become fewer and farther between. The darkness returns.**

> **Yet something has changed. The darkness no longer feels empty. It feels as though the cavern is watching.**

> **Ahead, the passage divides around a great pillar of black stone. You continue onward.**

> **An hour later, you see the same pillar. The same crack runs down its side. The same pale fungus grows from the same crevice. And scratched into the stone is a mark you recognize.**

> **Your own mark.**

The characters have somehow returned to the same location.

But they have been walking forward.

---

## **Investigation**

Allow the players to examine the area.

Useful discoveries:

* Their footprints don't match their current direction.  
* A piece of discarded material is still exactly where they left it.  
* A rope or chalk mark has somehow moved.  
* Magic reveals no conventional illusion.  
* A character who specifically examines the stone notices that **the stone itself appears to have changed**.  
* 

### **Reality Twist \#1**

Use an object the party possesses.

For example, a sword briefly appears ancient and rusted before returning to normal.

A torch flame burns sideways.

A healing potion briefly contains black liquid before becoming red again.

Don't explain it.

---

# **PART TWO \- THE STONE REMEMBERS**

The passage eventually enters a chamber where veins of pale **Godstone** run through the walls.

Fungal growth has begun spreading through the stone.

The characters encounter Myconids.

But unlike a conventional monster encounter:

## **The Myconids do not attack.**

They are working.

They cultivate fungal growth, remove dead material, and investigate the changes occurring around them.

They regard the adventurers with curiosity.

---

## **THE MYCONID MESSAGE**

Through rapport spores, gestures, images, and simple impressions, the Myconids communicate:

> **The stone is remembering.**

If questioned:

**What is happening?**

> "Something is changing."

**Is it evil?**

> "No."

**What is causing it?**

> "We do not know."

**Are you afraid?**

A long pause.

> "No."

> "We are interested."

Then:

> **"Nature changes."**

---

## **What the Myconids Know**

The Myconids have noticed:

* fungus growing where it shouldn't  
* creatures developing unfamiliar characteristics  
* passages changing  
* old stones revealing memories  
* areas that feel emotionally hostile  
* strange whispers traveling through the rock

They believe the Underdark is **adapting to an intrusion**.

They don't know what caused it.

They don't know where it will lead.

But they know something is learning.

---

# **PART THREE \- SPITE'S WHISPER**

As the characters leave the Myconids, begin introducing **Willpower Saves**.

Use Wisdom saves or whatever Willpower mechanic your ruleset provides.

Do this sparingly.

### **Trigger**

A character suddenly hears another party member say something cruel.

For example:

> "You know they don't trust you."

Or:

> "They're going to leave you behind."

Or:

> "You should have killed it."

The targeted character makes a save.

### **Success**

The character realizes the voice isn't coming from the other character.

They recognize something else:

**Someone is trying to influence them.**

### **Failure**

The character experiences a powerful impulse to attack the apparent source of the betrayal.

Do not remove their agency completely.

Instead, impose something like:

**Compulsion:** The character must spend their next action moving toward or attacking the perceived betrayer unless they use an appropriate action to resist.

Afterward, the character remembers what happened.

And knows:

> **Something wanted them to hurt their friends.**

---

# **PART FOUR**

![][image1]

# **THE NEST**

The characters enter a cavern where the walls have become strangely organic.

Fungal growth covers enormous cracks in the stone.

Something moves.

Then another shape.

### **COMBAT ENCOUNTER 1**

## **The Twisted Guardians**

Use **Hook Horrors** or similarly appropriate Underdark creatures from the group's miniature collection.

For seven 4th-level characters, use an appropriately challenging group of approximately:

* 2 Hook Horrors  
* several smaller cavern/fungal creatures as appropriate

The creatures are **not servants of Spite**.

They are protecting a strange growth at the heart of the cavern.

### **Environmental Effect**

At the end of each round, alter the battlefield slightly.

Examples:

* A passage narrows.  
* A fungal tendril emerges.  
* A stone shelf shifts.  
* The floor becomes slippery.  
* A wall opens into a previously unseen passage.

Avoid constant damage.

The environment is **moving**, not attacking.

---

## **After the Battle**

The characters discover that the creatures were protecting a mass of rapidly growing fungal tissue.

Inside it is something that looks almost like an embryo.

It is impossible to determine what creature it belongs to.

The Myconids would consider it:

> **"New life."**

The characters may destroy it.

Or leave it.

Neither choice is immediately punished.

This is important.

**The adventure isn't about fighting evil nature.**

It is about understanding something that is changing.

---

# **PART FIVE \- NO PLACE TO REST**

The characters continue.

Eventually they attempt to establish camp.

But the Underdark won't allow it.

## **The Moving Tunnel**

After several hours, someone notices:

The passage outside camp isn't the same passage.

The characters mark the walls.

They sleep.

When they wake:

**the marks are gone.**

Their campsite itself has shifted.

Not dramatically.

Perhaps only a few hundred feet.

But enough to prove something impossible:

> **The tunnels are moving.**

---

## **Long Rest Rule**

A long rest taken in an unprotected portion of the Twisted Path **does not provide its normal benefits**.

Short rests remain possible.

The characters can safely rest only in a location protected from the movement of the Path.

This creates a valuable reason to seek the Myconids again—or find another sanctuary.

---

# **PART SIX \- THE MEMORY-EATER**

![][image2]

Eventually the characters discover a small underground stream.

Beside it sits a creature.

A mind flayer.

It appears exhausted.

It doesn't immediately attack.

It looks at the characters.

### **Read-Aloud**

> **The creature slowly raises its head.**

> **Its eyes move from one face to another.**

> **For several seconds it says nothing.**

> **Then: "Oh."**

> **A pause.**

> **"You're real."**

**It studies them.**

> **"I wasn't sure anymore."**

This is the party's mysterious **mind flayer**.

---

## **THE BROKEN MEMORY**

The mind flayer doesn't remember:

* its name  
* exactly how long it has lived  
* where it came from  
* why it is in the Underdark  
* why certain places frighten it

But it remembers fragments.

He asks:

> **"Have you been hearing the chains?"**

If the characters ask what chains:

> "You will."

Then:

> **"Don't answer them."**

---

# **TRYING TO HEAL**

The mind flayer is wounded.

If the characters attempt to heal it, allow the healing to succeed.

But something extraordinary happens.

For a moment, **everyone sees the same memory**.

---

## **THE MEMORY**

> You stand somewhere impossibly deep.

> Stone surrounds you.

> Seven enormous shapes are hidden in darkness.

> You cannot see them clearly.

> You know only that they are vast.

> Something ancient is imprisoned here.

> Chains disappear into the darkness.

> A voice whispers:

> **"Remember us."**

> Then the vision ends.

The mind flayer looks at the party.

For one terrible moment, it remembers.

> **"I know this place."**

Then its expression changes.

Confusion.

Fear.

> "No."

> "I don't."

The memory is gone again.

---

# **PART SEVEN \-THE THING THAT REMEMBERS**

The healing has disturbed something nearby.

The characters encounter an apparently ordinary **Ooze**.

At first.

---

# **COMBAT ENCOUNTER 2**

## **THE REMEMBERING THING**

### **Phase One — The Ooze**

The creature behaves like a conventional ooze.

It:

* crawls across walls  
* extends pseudopods  
* attempts to engulf prey  
* absorbs organic and inorganic material

But during the battle, it begins to change.

Whenever it takes significant damage, describe something new.

A lump forms.

An eye appears.

A finger.

A tooth.

A face.

Then:

> A voice.

One of the characters hears their own voice.

> **"Remember when you left me?"**

Another mouth opens.

> **"Help me."**

Another:

> **"Don't go."**

None of the voices belong to anyone currently present.

---

## **PHASE TWO — THE MOUSER**

The Ooze begins violently convulsing.

Mouths tear open across its body.

Eyes appear.

Its amorphous body develops malformed limbs.

The Ooze has **become something resembling a Gibbering Mouther**.

But this creature is different.

### **It speaks memories.**

Not gibberish.

**Memories.**

The characters may hear:

* something one of them said earlier that day  
* a private conversation  
* a childhood phrase  
* a voice they recognize  
* something they haven't thought about in years

Then one final mouth opens.

> **"Remember us."**

---

## **Special Ability — Memory Harvest**

Whenever the creature successfully affects a character, the DM may reveal a brief sensory fragment from that character's past.

Keep it short.

One sentence.

One image.

One smell.

One sound.

Don't reveal anything important unless the player wants to develop it.

The creature isn't necessarily *reading* memories.

It is **absorbing them**.

---

# **THE CREATURE'S DEATH**

When finally destroyed:

> The creature collapses.

> The mouths disappear.

> The eyes close.

> The strange body melts into a dark puddle.

> Silence returns.

> Then you notice something lying in the residue.

> A small object.

> Something old.

> Something ordinary.

> And somehow...

> **familiar.**

Choose an object that can later become meaningful.

The players don't need to know why.

---

# **PART EIGHT \- THE LAST PATH**

The characters eventually reach three passages.

All three appear identical.

The mind flayer is with them—or has given them one final warning.

He stares at the passages.

> "Don't choose the safest one."

He looks confused.

> "I don't know why I know that."

The characters choose.

They travel.

Eventually they look back.

### **All three tunnels are behind them.**

The characters have somehow walked into a place where **the paths themselves have moved**.

---

# **FINAL SCENE \- THE STARLESS SEA**

**The passage finally opens.**

**And for the first time since entering the Underdark, the characters are not enclosed by walls.**

**They stand upon an immense stone overlook.**

**Far below lies the Starless Sea.**

**Black water stretches beyond sight.**

And there are strange lights at the top of the cavern. A strange tower coming from the roof. It doesn't appear quite straight.

**This is the destination of the next adventure.**

The Tower That Spite Built.

---

# **THE FINAL REVELATION**

**Then something moves beneath the Starless Sea.**

**Not a creature they can identify.**

**Something vast.**

**The water shifts.**

**A distant chain rises from the darkness.**

**CLANK.**

**The sound travels through the cavern.**

Then again.

**CLANK.**

The mind flayer stares downward.

His expression changes.

> **"I remember."**

Silence.

> "We have to go."

The characters look toward Karrag-Dum.

Then down toward the darkness beneath the Sea.

### **End Adventure.**

---

# **TREASURE & REWARDS**

This adventure is intentionally light on conventional treasure.

Instead, give the characters **knowledge**.

### **Possible discoveries**

**Memory Fragment**  
A character gains a mysterious memory that doesn't seem to belong to them.

**Myconid Spore Token**  
A small fungal growth that allows limited communication with Myconids or serves as proof that the party has been accepted by them.

**Godstone Fragment**  
A tiny piece of the strange stone. It occasionally becomes warm when reality shifts.

**The Mind Flayer's Warning**  
A phrase that may become important later:

> **"Don't answer the chains."**

### **The adventure's central line**

If there's one sentence I would keep in front of you while running the session, it's this:

> **"The Underdark isn't becoming evil. It's becoming something else."**

That gives **The Twisted Path** its own identity while setting up *The Tower That Spite Built* beautifully.
