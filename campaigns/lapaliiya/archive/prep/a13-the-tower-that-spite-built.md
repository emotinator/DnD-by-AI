Absolutely. I think this version of **Adventure 13 — The Tower That Spite Built** can become the real bridge between the horror of Episodes 11–12 and the wonder of Episodes 14–15.

The key change I'd make is that **the cyclopes don't merely appear at the end**. They become the unexpected guides who get the characters across the Sunless Sea—and the **jiggly, unstable platform** becomes a memorable adventure in its own right.

And underneath everything, the players gradually discover:

> **The Tower was never built by Spite.**

> Spite found something ancient and learned how to corrupt it.

---

# **ADVENTURE 13**

# **THE TOWER THAT SPITE BUILT**

### **A D\&D Adventure for 6 Characters of 4th Level**

**Estimated Play Time:** 4–5 hours  
**Location:** The shores of the Sunless Sea  
**Primary Threat:** Zohir Totek  
**Themes:** Discovery • Memory • Gravity • Ancient Power • Reality Distortion

---

## **ADVENTURE SUMMARY**

After surviving the Twisted Path, the adventurers emerge onto the shores of the Sunless Sea.

Across the impossible underground ocean stands an enormous structure suspended from the cavern ceiling.

The characters have no obvious way to reach it.

They encounter a small group of **cyclops pilgrims**, traveling across the sea to an ancient sacred site. The cyclopes agree to carry the adventurers across the water.

At the far side, the party discovers an ancient **gravity-transfer platform**—a strange, flexible stone mechanism that connects the sea to the ceiling.

Getting onto it is only the beginning.

The platform moves.

It bends.

It twists.

It changes gravity.

And eventually, it launches the characters upward toward the Tower.

Inside the Tower, the characters discover evidence that the structure predates every civilization they know.

It was one of **seven ancient binding stations**.

Spite did not build it.

Spite **corrupted it**.

The party eventually confronts **Zohir Totek**, who has discovered the Tower's true purpose and believes he can control the power within it.

The battle destabilizes the Tower.

The characters escape.

And as they look across the Sunless Sea, they finally see the destination of Episode 15:

**the Temple of Primordial Fire.**

---

# **PART ONE**

# **THE SUNLESS SEA**

### **Read Aloud**

> The passage behind you disappears into darkness.

> Ahead...

> there is nothing.

> No wall.

> No tunnel.

> No chamber.

> The darkness simply falls away.

> You step forward.

> And suddenly you understand.

> You are standing at the edge of an ocean.

> An ocean beneath the earth.

> It stretches farther than your eyes can follow.

> The water is black, but tiny lights move beneath its surface.

> Above you, thousands of points of blue and green light glitter in the darkness.

> For a moment, they look like stars.

> Then one pulses.

> Another answers.

> They are crystals.

> Enormous crystals hanging from a ceiling so distant it has become a sky.

> Great mushrooms rise from distant islands.

> Something enormous moves beneath the water.

> And somewhere in the darkness...

> you hear an oar strike the sea.

> **Splash.**

> **Splash.**

> **Splash.**

Give the players a moment.

This should be one of the great visual reveals of the campaign.

---

# **ENCOUNTER 1**

# **THE PILGRIMS OF THE DEEP**

A small boat approaches.

It is enormous by human standards.

Three cyclopes sit aboard it.

They are not warriors.

They are **pilgrims**.

Their clothing is simple and weathered. Each carries a small stone token around their neck.

![][image1]

Their leader is:

### **OGRUN STONE-EYE**

An elderly cyclops with a wooden oar almost twice the height of a human.

He is initially cautious.

But he isn't hostile.

His first question:

> "Surface folk?"

If the characters explain themselves, Ogrun replies:

> "Then you are very far from home."

### **The Pilgrims**

There are five cyclopes in total.

They are traveling to an ancient shrine across the Sunless Sea.

They call the destination:

### **The Place Above the Water**

They don't know its true history.

They simply believe it is sacred.

This is important.

The cyclopes should **not know everything**.

They are locals.

They know the sea.

They know its dangers.

They know which waters to avoid.

But the ancient structure is older than their people.

---

# **The Cyclopes' Offer**

Ogrun eventually offers:

> "We are going that way."

> He points across the enormous black sea.

> "If your feet can stay in the boat, you may come."

This creates a wonderful change of pace.

The characters don't have to fight their way across the Sunless Sea.

They **ride with the cyclopes**.

---

# **ENCOUNTER 2**

# **THE PILGRIM'S VOYAGE**

This isn't a combat encounter.

It's an opportunity for roleplaying and worldbuilding.

As they cross the Sunless Sea, the characters see things they have never imagined.

### **Possible sights**

Roll or choose:

**1\. The Moving Reef**

A glowing reef rises from the water.

It slowly moves.

It is actually a colony of enormous translucent creatures.

---

**2\. The Distant Boats**

Other cyclops boats travel across the darkness.

Some carry fishing nets.

Others carry strange cargo.

The Sunless Sea is not empty.

It is a highway.

---

**3\. The Deep**

Something enormous passes beneath the boat.

Nobody sees what it is.

Ogrun simply says:

> "Don't knock on the boat."

If asked why:

> "It hears."

---

**4\. The Drowned Ruins**

Half-submerged structures appear beneath the surface.

They aren't dwarven.

They aren't elven.

They aren't anything recognizable.

One cyclops quietly touches his pilgrimage stone.

---

**5\. The Tower**

Eventually the characters see it.

At first they think it is a mountain.

Then they realize:

**It is hanging from the ceiling.**

---

# **THE TOWER**

### **Read Aloud**

> Far across the sea...

> something hangs from the cavern roof.

> At first it looks like a mountain turned upside down.

> Then the lights reveal towers.

> Bridges.

> Spires.

> Vast stone chambers.

> All hanging downward from the ceiling.

> Nothing supports them from below.

> Beneath the entire structure is only black water.

> It is impossibly old.

> And somehow...

> it is still alive.

> A faint pulse of light moves through it.

> Once.

> Then again.

> Like a heartbeat.

Ogrun refuses to go directly underneath it.

Instead he takes them to an ancient platform.

---

# **ENCOUNTER 3**

# **THE JIGGLY PLATFORM**

This is the part I **really** like.

The platform should look ridiculous for about three seconds.

Then become terrifying.

It is an ancient circular slab of pale stone floating just above the water.

It looks solid.

Until someone steps on it.

### **Read Aloud**

> The first foot touches the platform.

> Nothing happens.

> Then the stone moves.

> Not sinks.

> Not rises.

> **Moves.**

> It flexes beneath your foot like something alive.

> The entire platform gives a slow, enormous wobble.

> The cyclops behind you says:

> "Ah."

> He pauses.

> "It still does that."

> The platform suddenly tilts thirty degrees.

> Someone's feet leave the ground.

> And then...

> it tilts back.

This becomes a mini skill challenge.

---

# **THE ASCENSION PLATFORM**

The ancient builders apparently created a mechanism for moving between the Sunless Sea and the ceiling.

The platform doesn't use stairs.

It uses **gravity**.

When activated, it changes the direction of gravity for everything standing upon it.

The characters will effectively **fall upward**.

But the mechanism is damaged.

Hence:

### **The Jiggle.**

---

## **Skill Challenge: Don't Fall Off the World**

Require **6 successes before 3 failures**.

Players can describe whatever they want.

Good possibilities:

* Athletics — brace themselves  
* Acrobatics — stay balanced  
* Arcana — understand the mechanism  
* Investigation — identify stable sections  
* Perception — anticipate movement  
* Nature — recognize how the platform responds  
* Religion — recognize ancient symbols  
* Thieves' Tools — stabilize a damaged mechanism  
* Strength — hold another character  
* Mage Hand / similar magic — manipulate controls

Reward creative solutions.

---

## **Platform Complications**

On a failure, roll or choose:

### **1\. Sudden Tilt**

Everyone slides several feet.

### **2\. Gravity Reversal**

For three seconds, everyone falls toward the ceiling.

Then gravity returns.

### **3\. Stone Ripple**

The platform ripples like water.

Everyone makes a Dexterity save.

### **4\. Floating Debris**

Pieces of ancient stone suddenly rise from the sea.

### **5\. The Platform Splits**

A crack opens.

It doesn't break.

But it moves.

### **6\. Something Below**

Something enormous strikes the underside.

**BOOM.**

The platform jumps.

Nobody sees what hit it.

---

# **THE ASCENT**

Once activated:

> The platform shudders.

> The water below begins to fall away.

> No.

> **You are falling upward.**

> The Sunless Sea drops beneath you.

> The cavern becomes a vertical world.

> Your stomach rises into your throat.

> The Tower grows larger.

> And suddenly you realize:

> the platform isn't carrying you.

> **Gravity is.**

This is a fantastic place for a visual.

The characters are suspended between:

**Sunless Sea below**

and

**Tower above.**

---

# **THE SEVEN CHAINS**

During the ascent, they see them.

Seven enormous chains.

They emerge from the Tower.

They disappear into the cavern ceiling.

One is broken.

One glows faintly.

The other five vanish into darkness.

Nobody explains them.

Ogrun doesn't know what they are.

The characters simply see them.

This is the first concrete evidence that the Tower is part of something much larger.

---

# **ENCOUNTER 4**

# **THE HANGING GATE**

The platform arrives at the Tower.

The characters find an enormous entrance.

There is no door.

Instead, the stone opens like a flower.

Inside is complete darkness.

The platform begins descending.

If the characters don't get off:

**they go back down.**

This creates a fun moment of urgency.

Once everyone is inside, the stone closes behind them.

---

# **PART TWO**

# **THE TOWER**

The Tower is not a normal dungeon.

It should feel like a **machine pretending to be a temple**.

And occasionally:

### **It moves.**

Walls breathe.

Floors tilt.

Doorways shift.

Gravity becomes uncertain.

The Tower isn't necessarily attacking them.

It is **malfunctioning**.

---

# **LOCATION 1**

# **THE THRESHOLD**

Seven symbols appear on the floor.

Six are dark.

One glows.

The characters may recognize the same symbol they saw during the ascent.

If touched:

> Something remembers you.

Each character briefly sees a memory from their own past.

Nothing dangerous.

Just personal.

Then the memory disappears.

This establishes the Tower's connection to **memory**.

---

# **LOCATION 2**

# **THE RESONANCE GALLERIES**

The characters enter a long corridor.

Their footsteps echo.

But the echoes don't repeat what they just said.

They repeat things they said **earlier in the campaign**.

Perhaps:

> "Remember us."

Or:

> "Don't answer them."

Or something one character said to another hours earlier.

Then the corridor repeats something none of them remember saying.

> "We were seven."

---

# **ENCOUNTER 5**

# **THE RESONANT GUARDIANS**

Here I'd use creatures from your collection rather than introducing another complicated monster.

The Tower has created distorted guardians from whatever has entered it.

For six level-4 characters, I'd use:

* 2–3 Hook Horrors or equivalent  
* plus a strange environmental effect

The creatures aren't fully themselves.

Their bodies occasionally freeze.

Their heads turn toward empty spaces.

Their claws seem fused into the stone.

When one takes damage, it occasionally whispers:

> "Remember."

### **Environmental Action**

At initiative count 20:

**Gravity shifts.**

Everyone must make a Dexterity save or slide 10 feet toward one wall.

This keeps the encounter interesting without simply inflating hit points.

---

# **LOCATION 3**

# **THE MEMORY VAULT**

This is one of the most important rooms.

There are no books.

No inscriptions.

Instead, the walls contain **memories**.

Touching a section causes a vision.

The characters see seven figures.

Ancient.

Different.

Standing around something enormous.

The figures are performing a ritual.

Chains form.

The vision ends.

Another memory.

The seven figures are exhausted.

One says:

> "We cannot destroy them."

Another:

> "Then bind them."

And another:

> "Seven places."

The vision ends.

The players now understand:

### **The Tower is one of seven.**

But they still don't know exactly what is being imprisoned.

---

# **LOCATION 4**

# **THE BROKEN ENGINE**

The party reaches the heart of the Tower.

This is where Spite has done his work.

Ancient machinery has been altered.

Black veins run through the walls.

Reality bends around them.

Objects occasionally appear a few inches away from where they were placed.

The Tower's original energy is being redirected.

This is the source of the corruption.

And here the characters find evidence of **Zohir Totek**.

His notes.

His tools.

His experiments.

He has been studying the Tower.

And he has discovered what it is.

---

# **ZOHIR'S REVELATION**

Before the final fight, give Zohir a chance to talk.

He should be intelligent.

Not ranting.

Not cartoonishly evil.

He knows something the party doesn't.

> "You think this place was built to contain something."

> He smiles.

> "You're wrong."

> "It was built to contain **what was already here.**"

He gestures toward the walls.

> "The people who made this place didn't understand what they were dealing with."

> "Neither did Spite."

> "But I..."

> "I am beginning to."

---

# **ENCOUNTER 6**

# **ZOHIR TOTEK**

### **Boss Battle**

For six level-4 characters, I would make Zohir a **mobile controller/skirmisher**, rather than simply a sack of hit points.

His greatest weapon is the Tower.

---

## **PHASE ONE — THE SCHOLAR**

Zohir fights intelligently.

He uses:

* psychic/force attacks  
* teleportation or short-range movement  
* the environment  
* cover  
* the Tower's machinery

He should constantly move.

He knows the room.

The characters don't.

---

## **Tower Actions**

Once each round, the Tower does something.

### **Gravity Shift**

One section of the room becomes a wall.

### **Memory Echo**

A character hears a voice from their past.

They must make a Wisdom save or become distracted until their next turn.

### **Broken Platform**

A piece of floor rotates.

### **Energy Pulse**

A beam of ancient energy strikes an area.

The point isn't to make the battle lethal.

It's to make the players feel like:

> **They're fighting inside something much bigger than themselves.**

---

# **PHASE TWO — THE TOWER WAKES**

At approximately half Zohir's hit points:

> Zohir looks toward the ceiling.

> "No."

> For the first time...

> he looks afraid.

> "That isn't supposed to happen."

> The Tower moves.

The room rotates.

The chains outside begin moving.

The glowing symbol beneath the Tower suddenly becomes brilliant.

And Zohir realizes he has activated something he cannot control.

---

## **Zohir Draws on the Binding**

His attacks become stronger.

But the Tower begins breaking around him.

Now the characters have two objectives:

### **Defeat Zohir.**

### **Survive the Tower.**

This makes the climax much more memorable than a normal boss fight.

---

# **THE DEATH OF ZOHIR**

When Zohir finally falls:

> Zohir collapses.

> The machinery continues.

> The Tower does not stop.

> For several seconds, there is only silence.

> Then one of the seven lights outside goes dark.

> Far below...

> something moves.

> And you hear it.

> **CLANK.**

> A chain.

Nobody knows what it means.

Yet.

---

# **THE ESCAPE**

The Tower is becoming unstable.

The party has to return to the gravity platform.

But now the platform is **much worse**.

It is damaged by the Tower's collapse.

This becomes the final quick skill challenge.

### **4 successes before 2 failures.**

The players must:

* stabilize the platform  
* leap across gaps  
* manipulate gravity  
* catch falling companions  
* avoid falling debris

Eventually:

> The platform drops.

> The Tower disappears above you.

> The cavern becomes enormous again.

> The Sunless Sea rushes toward you.

> And then...

> **SPLASH.**

The cyclopes are waiting.

---

# **THE FINAL SCENE**

The cyclopes are silent.

They don't ask what happened.

Ogrun looks upward.

Then across the sea.

> "You went inside."

He looks at the characters.

> "What did you find?"

Before they can answer, something catches their attention.

Far across the Sunless Sea.

Something enormous glows in the darkness.

Not the Tower.

Something else.

The cyclops slowly lowers his oar.

> "Ah."

He looks genuinely unsettled.

> "The old temple."

---

# **ENDING IMAGE**

The characters look across the Sunless Sea.

And there it is.

Far away.

Hanging from the cavern ceiling.

Vast.

Ancient.

Beautiful.

Nothing like the Tower.

Its surfaces glow with an inner fire.

No right angles.

No recognizable architecture.

It looks less like a building than something that **grew**.

And beneath it:

Nothing.

Just miles of black water.

Ogrun speaks quietly:

> "We don't go there."

A pause.

> "Not anymore."

### **END ADVENTURE 13**

---

# **THE SECRET DM TRUTH**

The players should **not** know all of this yet.

### **What actually happened**

The Tower is one of the **Seven Bindings**.

It was created by an ancient civilization that existed before the known peoples of Toril.

The seven stations were designed to contain the Dawn Titans.

The Tower's original purpose was not destructive.

It was a **stabilizing anchor**.

Spite discovered it much later.

He corrupted the station and learned how to manipulate its energies.

Zohir discovered Spite's work and went further.

He believed the Binding could be **controlled**.

He was wrong.

---

## **The really important distinction**

**Spite didn't build the Tower.**

He built the **corruption inside it**.

That means the title *The Tower That Spite Built* becomes slightly misleading.

And I love that.

The players won't realize the joke until much later.

---

# **EPISODE 13 → 14 → 15**

This now gives us a really clean three-part progression:

### **Episode 13**

**The Tower That Spite Built**

> *We have discovered something ancient.*

### **Episode 14**

**The Sunless Sea**

> *There is an entire world down here.*

### **Episode 15**

**The Temple of Primordial Fire**

> *This world is older than we ever imagined.*

And the final visual of Episode 13 is especially important because it gives the players a **destination** for Episode 15 without simply saying, "Here's the next dungeon."

They have just survived the Tower.

They are exhausted.

They look across the Sunless Sea.

And for the first time, the thing waiting for them doesn't look frightening.

It looks **sacred**.

That is the perfect transition from **horror → wonder → awe**.
