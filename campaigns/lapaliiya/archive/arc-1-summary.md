# Arc 1 Summary — The Hawklin Decoy

_Written 2026-10-19 from Nelgron's event log, Parts 1–4 (Entries 1–~24; 16 and 17 reserved, empty). Party level 1–3. Session files exist only for `sessions/arc1-s1-the-forest-that-laughs.md` and `sessions/arc1-s2-ember-throne.md`._

## Origins and the escort road
- **Before play:** Nelgron, a worn-out con artist, joins the Ember Quiet monastery out of exhaustion, not faith.
- **The Blizzard:** she is transported from the monastery into a mountain blizzard with no memory of leaving. She meets the other PCs there, and they fight off a threat from the ice.
- **Lapaliiya (Lapadiiya):** the party emerges together at the trade city's gates. Nelgron works honestly for two weeks.
- **Escort contract:** a minor noble, a security detail, merchant wagons. The noble is "the princess," in truth the decoy cousin.
  - Wolf-riding goblins driven off. A dire wolf nearly kills Nelgron (permanent thigh scar).
  - **Bog troll:** Nelgron deserts for treasure, comes back, revives the Druid, and is struck down. Radiant magic finishes the troll.
  - **Enemy camp:** 60+ watchers on the road. The party kills the leader and lieutenants in their sleep; the camp scatters, and so does the party.
  - **Giants, cove, banshee:** giants block the road for a gem from a cove. A bound banshee in a crypt asks to be freed on her own terms; the party refuses, then frees her soul. Gem returned; passage granted.
  - **Blizzard ambush:** a guard killed by a giant's spear. The party looses the oxen as a diversion and shelters in a temple sunk into a hillside.
  - **The Temple Below the Snow:** unnatural shadows, old traps, a blind fight in magical darkness. The party cleanses a desecrated moon pool. (Prep: `archive/prep/lapaliiya-shattered-sanctum.md`, a Selûnite temple defiled by Shar.)
  - **The chasm:** a gnoll priestess performs a ritual across a chasm opening onto the Underdark. Killed from range; her gnolls finished off.

## The city and the princess plot
- **The Spire Bull** rampages through the market; Nelgron protects civilians.
- An elderly Ilmateri monk: *"Suffering has chosen this road… who will walk it without bitterness."*
- **The princess is taken** again by fire giants: thirty escorts dead, seven giants among them; rumour says the giants were hired. The party agrees to help without pay.
- Nelgron sells the loot well. The party is careless and draws attention.
- **Inn ambush:** doors barred, the inn set on fire. One attacker dies on the ramparts. Note on the body: *"Forge Agreement… Mt. Aider contract."* The party leaves before dawn under suspicion.
- **The Forest that Laughs:** see `sessions/arc1-s1-the-forest-that-laughs.md`.
- **The Ember Throne (Kiln Hall):** see `sessions/arc1-s2-ember-throne.md`. The Priest's moon chant carries the party to Ormpetarr; the captive comes with them (table canon).

## The House That Bears (Nelgron solo)
- Nelgron finds the Elder of the Ember Quiet at an unmarked hospice for the dying. He gives her no teaching, only menial work.
- A **Loviataran cult** feeds on the patients. Nelgron heals a woman turning grey; **Warrior of Mercy** abilities manifest (level 3).
- The cult attacks openly. Nelgron takes a killing blow meant for the dying woman. The cult is driven off.
- The Elder gives her the **Bandaged Wraps of Humility**.
- Meanwhile the rest of the party learned the truth about the captive and took other work in Ormpetarr. **[prep]** Probably *The Lanterns Beneath Moonfall Court*; see `bible.md`.

## The Decree
- A posted decree: **King Haedrak III of Tethyr** marries **Lady Caralyn Hawklin**, Royal Consort, sealing the **Tethyr–Lapaliiya alliance**, witnessed by Tyr and Torm.
- **The truth:** the girl the party escorted and freed was a **decoy cousin**, also called Caralyn. Amn manipulated the fire giants to stop the alliance. The party was cheap, expendable cover. The decoy, her purpose spent, travels with the party. She may be a loose end.
- The party heads north by the Emerald Way on Seressa's map. Arc 2 begins (`log-recent.md`).
