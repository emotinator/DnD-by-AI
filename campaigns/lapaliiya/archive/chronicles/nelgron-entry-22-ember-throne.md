# Nelgron Illfigure — Event Log: Adder Peaks to Kiln Hall
_(Session behind chronicle Entry 22, "Ember Throne." Player-provided; kept verbatim as a source.)_

1. Crossing the Adder Peaks
- The party pursues the captive (the "hawkling girl") into the Adder Peaks. Snow is knee-deep.
- Figures are spotted at a distance. The party splits on how to read them, and nobody coordinates.
- The Goliath breaks for high ground alone and comes back under arrow fire.

2. Avalanche ambush
- An avalanche splits the line. The Druid's thunder spell sets off a second slide.
- Nelgron crosses a fallen tree to reach the cut-off side.
- The Druid ends up buried head-down among enemy dead. Nelgron digs him out before the cold kills him.
- Enemies: two killed, two fled. Their gear is looted.
- Recovered note: "Transfer the hawkling asset before the ember feast."
- Enemy colours were black and red. A Mage was among the ambushers; he turns up again at the embassy.

3. The volcano approach
- On the slope, the party sees chained men hauling basalt by wagon, overseen by a small Fire Giant.
- The party watches and moves on without intervening.

4. The basalt city and reconnaissance
- The city is built where the mountain opens: black basalt spires and a temple to Surtur.
- Markets and slave-pens run side by side, and Azer work the slave trade.
- The Druid finds a cooled lava tube. The party uses it as a hidden base for several days of watching the city.

5. Embassy infiltration
- The party comes up through the lava tube into the closed market, then reaches the embassy walls.
- They overhear the Mage from the ambush talking with a soft-spoken, well-dressed Halfling. Once the girl is secured, the giants' mining rights will be enforced.
- The Ranger and Druid get inside disguised as servants and bring documents out:
  - Council of Five treaty fragments, which mention the Territory of Amn and the "Ember Throne". Article Five: the individual is to be secured and held until territorial guarantees are met.
  - A letter to a Factor, which:
    - calls the giants "useful but predictably prideful"
    - says recognition should be delayed until excavation quotas are met
    - says rivalry among the forge clans should be kept going
    - says the Embercrown line must never gain coastal access
    - notes that replacement claimants have been identified in case the queen proves difficult
- The Mage says he isn't sure he didn't leave her at Kiln Hall. The party heads there.

6. Kiln Hall
- Layout: chain catwalks over a floor split by lava. Guards are posted at the door. A Huge giant is working at something in the centre, and the captive is held against the back wall.
- The party splits: some go over the catwalks, some go through the door.
- Two Azer at the front escalate the fight quickly.
- The Priest goes down near the lava gap.
- The Huge giant turns on the smaller Fire Giant and smashes it, apparently an internal feud.
- Nelgron descends the chain, striking the Fire Giant on the way down.
- The Druid is hit from below and falls from the upper chain.
- Nelgron reaches the Priest and revives him.
- Nelgron is then struck down and knocked unconscious.

7. The carrying
- Nelgron comes to and finds the party running toward the Priest, who is chanting something old with a "moon" quality to it.
- Moonlight comes through a window in the fire city.
- The party is suddenly in Ormpetarr, with no transition anyone can explain.

8. Aftermath
- The party is home. Per Entry 22, the captive was not brought back.
- Nelgron resolves to find an Elder of the Ember Quiet who can explain what carried them. This leads into the House That Bears arc.
