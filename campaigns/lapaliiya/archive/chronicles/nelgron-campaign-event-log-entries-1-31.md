## Quick Reference

**Player character:** Nelgron Illfigure, a 38-year-old tiefling. She spent 30+ years as a charlatan and con artist, and is now a monk of the Ember Quiet (an Ilmateri order).
- **Subclass:** Warrior of Mercy, level 3. The level-4 advancement is pending; the DM asked for four "magnificent feats/saves" drawn from her history to justify it.

## Part 1 — Origins and the Escort Road

### Before play: Monastery initiation
- Nelgron arrives at the Ember Quiet Monastery out of exhaustion, not faith.
- She gives her real name and trains in breath, restraint and the body.

### The Blizzard
- Nelgron is somehow transported from the monastery into a mountain blizzard, with no memory of leaving.
- She meets the other PCs there, strangers caught in the same storm, and they fight off a threat from the ice.

### Lapadiiya
- The party emerges together at the gates of the trade city of Lapadiiya.
- Nelgron spends two weeks there doing honest labour (carrying crates, guarding doors), resisting the urge to work the markets.

### Escort contract
- The party takes an escort job: a minor noble travelling with a small security detail and merchant wagons.
- **Goblins riding wolves** attack and are driven off.
- **A dire wolf** nearly kills Nelgron. The bite leaves her with a **permanent thigh injury**.

### The Bog Troll
- Nelgron **abandons the party** mid-fight to go after treasure.
- She hears the screams, returns, **revives the Druid**, and draws the troll's attention. She is struck down.
- Another party member finishes the troll with radiant magic.

### The Enemy Camp
- A large force (60+) is waiting on the road, watching and counting wagons. It is led by an abusive, feared leader.
- The party infiltrates at night and **kills the leader and several lieutenants in their sleep**.
- The leaderless camp scatters, and so does the party.

### The Giants, the Cove, the Banshee
- Giants block the road and demand that the party fetch a gem from a cove they cannot reach.
- In a poorly sealed crypt beyond the cove, a **bound banshee** asks to be freed *on her own terms*.
- The party refuses, then ends her unrest and frees her soul.
- They return the gem, and the giants let them pass.

### Blizzard Caravan Ambush
- A second blizzard hits. The party circles the wagons, with large shapes moving outside the firelight.
- A guard is killed by a giant-sized spear.
- The party **looses the oxen into the storm as a diversion** and shelters in a temple sunk into a hillside.

### The Temple Below the Snow
- The shadows inside behave unnaturally.
- The **princess** (the escort's charge) grows frantic. The party's priest offers her comfort he doesn't believe.
- Old traps line the descent. At the bottom, the party fights unseen things in **magical darkness**; Nelgron fights blind.
- At the heart of the temple, the party **cleanses a desecrated moon pool**.

### The Chasm and the Priestess
- Beyond the temple, a **gnoll priestess** performs a ritual across a deep chasm opening onto the Underdark, with gnolls on the party's side.
- The party attacks from range. The priestess dies, and the leaderless gnolls are finished off.

> **Gap:** Entries 16 and 17 are intentionally reserved slots. There is no record for them.

## Part 2 — The City and the Princess Plot

### The Spire Bull
- A creature rampages through the city market.
- Nelgron prioritises protecting civilians over fighting the creature.

### The Monk's Words
- In the east market, an elderly Ilmateri monk says in passing: *"Suffering has chosen this road. It always does. The question is who will walk it without bitterness."*
- This is a character beat, not a plot event, but it is a recurring touchstone for the player.

### The Messenger
- Messengers report that the **princess has been taken** and ask for the party's help. No payment is offered.
- The party agrees.

### Before the Woods
- **Fire Giants** took the princess again: thirty escorts dead, seven giants among them. City rumour says the giants were hired.
- Nelgron sells the party's loot using her old skills, and gets a better-than-fair price for the group.
- The party is careless, buying obvious supplies and asking telling questions, and draws attention.
- **Inn ambush:**
  - Oak timbers bar the party's doors from outside, and the inn is set on fire.
  - The party escapes and fights assailants on the ramparts. One attacker dies; the rest escape.
  - **Note on the body:** *"Forge Agreement… Mt. Aider contract."*
- The party **leaves the city before dawn under suspicion**.

### Chondalla Woods ("The Forest that Laughs")
- The forest is actively hostile: shifting paths and disorientation.
- **Drunk satyrs** block the trail. The Priest negotiates passage, which comes suspiciously easily.
- The party camps **without fire** for safety.
- **Night ambush:** hooved humanoid "puppets" made of wood are controlled by a poisonous **vine creature**. The party wins, badly battered.

### Adder Peaks and the Ember Throne (the Kiln Hall session)
**The Adder Peaks**
- In knee-deep snow, distant figures appear and the party fractures on how to read them. The Goliath breaks for high ground and returns under arrow fire.
- **Avalanche ambush** (made worse by the Druid's thunder spell). Nelgron crosses a fallen tree and digs the buried Druid out.
  - Enemies wore black and red. Two were killed and two fled; a **Mage** was among them.
  - **Note recovered:** *"Transfer the hawkling asset before the ember feast."*

**The city**
- On the volcano slope, the party passes chained men hauling basalt under a small Fire Giant and does not intervene.
- The **basalt Fire Giant city** has black spires, a **temple to Surtur**, slave-pens beside the markets, and **Azer** slavers.
- The party spends several days of reconnaissance from a cooled lava tube found by the Druid.

**Embassy infiltration**
- The party overhears the Mage and a soft-spoken **Halfling** discussing securing the "hawkling girl" in exchange for enforced giant mining rights.
- The Ranger and Druid get inside disguised as servants and recover documents:
  - **Council of Five treaty fragments** (Territory of Amn; "Ember Throne"; *Article Five:* the individual is held until territorial guarantees are met).
  - **A letter to a Factor**, which:
    - calls the giants "useful but predictably prideful"
    - says recognition should be delayed until excavation quotas are met
    - says forge-clan rivalry should be kept going
    - says the Embercrown line must be denied coastal access
    - notes that replacement claimants have been identified for the queen
- The Mage isn't sure he didn't leave the girl at **Kiln Hall**.

**Kiln Hall**
- **Layout:** chain catwalks over lava, guards at the door, a Huge giant in the centre, and the captive at the back wall.
- The party splits between the catwalks and the door. Two Azer escalate the fight.
- The Huge giant **turns on and smashes** the smaller Fire Giant, apparently an internal feud.
- The Priest falls near the lava. The Druid falls from the upper chain.
- Nelgron descends the chain striking the Fire Giant, **revives the Priest**, and is then knocked unconscious.
- The Priest chants something old and moon-scented. Moonlight appears, and the party is **inexplicably transported to Ormpetarr**.

**Outcome and contradiction**
- The journal says the captive was *not* brought home.

> **⚠ Continuity contradiction:** A later summary says the party "rescued" the girl from the Ember Throne, and she currently travels with them. When and how she was recovered is unresolved.

## Part 3 — The House That Bears (Nelgron solo arc; level 3)

- Nelgron seeks an Elder of the Ember Quiet to explain the transport at Kiln Hall.
- She finds him at a **hospice for the dying**, not a monastery. There is no signage; she locates it using her old people-finding skills.
- She feels nothing before the order's **martyr-relics**.
- The Elder gives her no teaching, only menial work: washing, carrying and binding the dying, with repetitive rules (*basin on the left; bind before you look at the face*).
- A **Loviataran cult** (pain inflicted for pleasure) is secretly feeding on the patients. One recovering woman turns grey, her pain unnaturally sharpened.
- Nelgron heals her. **Warrior of Mercy abilities manifest here** (level 3).
- **Cult assault:** the cult attacks the hospice openly at night.
  - Nelgron **takes a killing blow meant for the dying woman**, forgoing a clean kill to shield her.
  - The cult is driven off.
- The Elder gives her the **Bandaged Wraps of Humility**: *"These don't keep you safe. They keep what you give."*

> The rest of the party was elsewhere during this arc. They learned the truth about the captive and took other work in Ormpetarr.

## Part 4 — The Decree and the Northern Road

### The Royal Decree
- A posted decree announces the marriage of **King Haedrak III of Tethyr** to **Lady Caralyn Hawklin**, Royal Consort. It seals the **Tethyr–Lapaliiya alliance**, is witnessed by Tyr and Torm, and promises "peace in uncertain times."

> **Campaign fact (the Caralyn correction):** The girl the party escorted was a **decoy cousin**, also called Caralyn. She was bait so that hunters would waste themselves on the wrong target. The real Caralyn married the king.
> - **Agents of Amn** manipulated the Fire Giants to prevent the alliance.
> - The party was **cheap, expendable cover**.
> - The decoy now travels with the party, her purpose spent. She is possibly a loose end.

### North by the Emerald Way
- Seressa's map leads the party north through **millennia-dead cities**: no signs of war or fire, only abandonment.

### Mussum
- A still-standing **Ilmateri hospice**. Its monks suffer memory loss and forget their own days.
- A mural shows **seven lantern-bearers: six extinguished, one lit low**.
- **Brother Malven** gives the party a portion of the last flame. It goes dull and ordinary once they leave the city.

### Shamph
- The dead city feels **watched**.
- The **Temple of Tyr** is roofless. Beneath it, archives have been worn blank: erased, not burned.
- An unlit lantern there is inscribed ***"Justice Remembers."***
- When the light fails, **memory-draining shadows** attack. They strip memories, including (for Nelgron) her sense of Ilmater. The memories return afterward, but not quite right.

### The road beyond Shamph
- **Riders** pass with no heraldry, no names and no colours.
- At **Maetherwatch / Maerthwatch**, an ancient construct bars the way, repeating *"State your watchword."* No one knows the word. How the party got past is not remembered.
- **Three weeks of lost time.** Afterward, Nelgron has:
  - an **unknown key** (heavy, notched, not dwarven-cut)
  - **journals in her own handwriting** that she doesn't remember writing, including the line *"Do not go back for it."*
  - depleted food and a lot of ground covered
- Six sensory fragments of the lost weeks exist in the player's notes but have not been used in play:
  - a blister ledger
  - mis-tied gems
  - a chess floor
  - a large shape at distance
  - a tower overlooking a town
  - writing at night

### Arrival at Davarn
- A thriving **Dwarven town devoted to Moradin**.
- A child says ***"Nothing comes from there"*** about the direction the party arrived from.

## Part 5 — Davarn

### One Keeper Returns
> Detail for this session is thinner in the hand-off material. Known points:
- **Mimon Tamberweed** is Davarn's **blind oracle and stone-white-eyed minister of Moradin**. These are one person holding two roles.
  - She read Nelgron's intent before Nelgron could act: *"Come to me on the morrow, and I shall answer all that you ask."*
- The **Festival of Moradin** is taking place. Nelgron suspected something beneath it.
- A **mine rescue** took place, with Nelgron prominent.
- **The Mussum flame was delivered.** One of the seven Keepers/lanterns "returned" at Davarn.
- The oracle told the decoy Caralyn: *"You are with the fire now."*
- **The Stone at Davarn:** a 100 ft standing stone that restores memory to miners who went too deep. It is a possible remedy for the lost weeks, not yet used.

### The King that Crawls
- **Street performance:** a troupe led by **Manco** performs, followed by a reception at **the Lucky Imp**.
- Manco hands the Priestess a **coin** and mentions being hunted.
- **Shadows attack:**
  - The Bard is poisoned; Nelgron heals him.
  - The Bard disintegrates a wall, and the Rogue glows like a torch.
  - A troupe member destroys one shadow with a beam of light. The second folds into energy and vanishes.
- **The coin's delivery chain:** it is to be delivered to **Factor Valance** on behalf of **Saressa**, a **Cowled Wizard**, who can speak through it.
  - The party also **owes Manco an exchange** of unknown contents.
- **Descent into the Underdark:**
  - Nelgron carries a month's rations.
  - After five hours down, the party reaches **colossal memorial doors** covered in carved names, flanked by stone dwarf sentinels and two braziers. Three panels read: *REMEMBER US / REMEMBER THOSE WHO WALKED BELOW / REMEMBER THOSE WHO DID NOT RETURN.*
- **The Keeper's question:**
  - A **Keeper** speaks in Common and asks a question.
  - He warns that anyone who fails to give **the same answer on return** has begun to be claimed by the dark.
  - He opens the gate with a key; it opens silently.
  - The question and answer have **deliberately never been written down**.
- **The vanishing:** the Priestess cracks a light crystal, and **eight went through, four are walking**. No one notices or remembers except Nelgron. The decoy Caralyn is among the missing.
- **The tunnel:** featureless, with no cover and no sound. Openings in the walls release creatures, and the glowing rock gives poor light.
  - A **worm** bursts from the wall and nearly swallows Nelgron; it is killed. Nelgron contracts **Corpse Rot**.
- **Further down:**
  - Chains are heard dragging in the dark.
  - Saressa speaks through the coin: *stay on the path, keep to the white stones.*
  - The party passes an abandoned camp. There is scratching overhead.
  - **The Ranger is dragged into the dark** and recovered with a grappling hook.
- **The fissure:** a warm mushroom cavern with giant bioluminescent fungi and running water.
  - Before sleep, a vision of **seven indistinct figures** below.

### The Twisted Path
- **The swap:** on waking, **some party members have been replaced**: different people, answering to the same names. No one cares, Nelgron included.
- **The loop:** the party walks in a **circle** and proves it only with a scratched marker.
- **Alternate route:** the Rogue finds an opening onto a route that goes against Saressa's warning. The Ranger goes first.
- **Fungal crab:** a giant carapace covered in glowing mushrooms, with spore clouds that scatter the party. It is killed messily.
- **The egg:** a warm, pulsing orb. Nelgron takes it as **a portent from Ilmater** and carries it.
- **The Mind Flayer at the oasis:** clean water and falls, with a **mind flayer** at rest, robed and non-hostile.
  - It asks whether the party is **real** and whether they hear **chains** (they do).
  - A shared memory fragment shows **seven figures** and the words ***"remember us."***
  - It describes something with **pale skin, dark features and an aura of death**, and wants to go back toward it.
  - **The Flayer joins the party.**
- **The myconid:** a myconid-type creature asks for the egg. Nelgron **trades it** for **broken white stones**.
  - It says: *"They have disturbed." / "They who cling to the ceiling." / "Something blocks the way." / "Those that have gone where you go, have not returned."*
- **The ooze:** after a rest, a northwest breeze brings the thought *"we are not alone."* The party finds itself standing on an **ooze**.
  - Nelgron destroys it with a palm strike. It leaves a **4-inch ornately carved wooden peg**.
- **The overlook:** following the breeze, the party reaches a stone overlook above a colossal cavern.
  - The cavern holds a still black lake, spired islands, and a **city built downward from the ceiling** with warm gold lights.
  - **Fungus is growing on Nelgron's backpack.**

## Part 6 — The Seven Chains *(sessions logged as notes; no journal entries yet)*

### The Temple that Spite Built
**At the overlook**
- While the party rests at the overlook, the **missing Priestess re-emerges** from another passage. She steps out of a shimmering shadow that ripples like heat-haze.
- **Kaitlin (the decoy Caralyn) is also with the party again.** She was among those who vanished in "The King that Crawls." How the missing returned is unexplained, consistent with the deliberately unreliable party count.

**The cyclopes**
- A **boat with three cyclopes** arrives and asks why the party is there.
  - They say the **lit structures hanging from the ceiling weren't there a fortnight ago**, and that the **sound of chains has grown louder**.
  - They're on a **pilgrimage deep into the Sunless Sea**, passing **"the Stone in the Water."**
- The cyclopes and the Mind Flayer ignore each other as if that were normal.
- Party members negotiate in Giant (Nelgron doesn't speak it) and secure a ride. **"The Stone in the Water" is the ceiling-hung city.** Travel stories are swapped en route.
- A note on naming: the cyclopes were once called "ogres"; it's the same group.

**The ascent**
- A **floating platform with pulsing runes** rises shakily and erratically toward the hanging structures. The party waves farewell to the cyclopes.
- A **Manticore** harasses the party from range with spikes as the platform rises.
- **Kaitlin blasts a lightning bolt** from the platform. No one knew she had any such ability.
- **Two floating bridges** run about 200 ft to a stable platform connected to the tower.
  - **Small 2-ft creatures** shoot from range and try to **cut the bridges** on the far side.
  - The party races across under fire and kills them just in time.
- In a **garden area**, a **shambling mound** of greenery and fungus attacks and is killed by the group.

**The seven chains**
- **Seven massive chains** rise into the dark: **only one glows**, a **different one has a broken link**, and the other five are dark.
- The **Priestess consults her patron**, describing the puzzle.
- **Puzzle:** an ascending staircase, solved by **stepping only on the glowing runes**.
- **Translucent rock-like humanoids** attack, two emerging from the floor. Nelgron shatters the last one with a punch.

**The Mind Flayer's memories**
- The **Mind Flayer** leads on, drawn by a **pull of memories** that aren't its own.
- **Vision:** seven figures stand around something enormous, with the chains present. *"We performed some kind of ritual."* Then: ***"We are exhausted. We cannot destroy it. We must bind it, in seven places."***
- **Hall of stories:** smells of earth and herbs, with chains audible.
  - The Flayer: ***"We are at one. Have I been at the others?"*** / ***"I don't think I've been to the other seven, no, no…"***

**Zohir and the tower's fall**
- In a downward chamber: wraiths, and the **pale-skinned, dark-featured, death-shrouded woman** the Flayer described earlier, named **Zohir**.
  - She speaks Undercommon, then Common: ***"You think it was built to contain something…"***
- **Fight:** the floor shifts and rotates, and wraiths and risen spectres strike with draining touches.
  - Kaitlin sporadically **blasts radiant energy**.
  - Nelgron strikes the floor near Zohir, weakening it and setting off a burst that **explodes a wraith**.
  - Zohir cries out, oozing death energy, and **fades away with her minions**. She was not confirmed killed.
- **The tower begins to collapse.** The party dashes back to the platform, badly hurt, and makes it.
- **Another chain breaks, and the tower drops.** Two of the seven bindings are now down.
- The platform descends to the waiting cyclopes.

### The Dawn Titans
**The ferry**
- The cyclopes ferry the party away, asking questions, and offer to take them to the **"Old Temple."**
- The hanging temple's remains **fall into the water** behind them. Someone may have glimpsed something deep in the water (unconfirmed).
- They approach a **partially sunken city** with a **lit archway** about 200 ft up the shore.
- A cyclops gives **every party member an identical stone set with a gem**. He speaks cryptically about choices and an uncertain fate, and says, looking at Kaitlin: ***"I sense this may be needed."***
- The party wades ashore through knee-deep water. **The Rogue flags something suspicious**, as he always does.

**The serpent-worm**
- A **giant serpent/worm** bites Nelgron's leg and attacks the group in the water.
- The party fights while retreating to shore and **escapes it; it was not killed**.

**The bronze door**
- After about 30 minutes into the flooded city, the party reaches a **warm bronze door**.
- It accepts the **gem-stones in a complex sequence**, which takes time to solve. The heat dissipates as it opens.
- **Inside:**
  - a chamber resembling **the forge at Davarn**
  - a **lantern**
  - a **table with inscriptions**
  - something moving
- **Floor runes (Primordial):** ***"When the first fire faltered, seven hands held the world together."***
- **The hero's lantern** reveals a **rune-inscribed creature/construct blocking a passage**. An **endless shaft** rises above the platform.
- Side passages hold a **meditation area**, a **sack**, and **scattered coins**.

**The temple**
- **Vision on entering:** seven figures of different shapes and sizes among **immense elemental beings**. **Chains of light spread through the earth** as the figures bind the beings. It feels ancient, and it ends before showing why.
- **Second vision:** **the seven stations**. One is the tower from the previous session, and at least one is shown broken.
  - ***"One of the bindings of the tower is failing."***
- **At the threshold:** everyone feels something is wrong and out of alignment. The heat returns, and the **floor cracks over magma**.
- **First room:** a vast amber-lit arched hall. An enormous root/tendril-like mass hangs from a glowing ring in the ceiling, ending in a toothed, maw-like dome. Ruined machinery and towers line the sides.
- **On approach:**
  - a colossal arch of wings framing **two stacked rune-wheels with a spiralling column of golden fire**
  - a sunburst-crowned carved face casting light onto a tall crowned/horned figure
  - crouching stone humanoids on both sides

**The altar fight**
- The **constructs activate**. They smash the **Mind Flayer** first (its fate afterward is unrecorded), then the party.
- **Kaitlin runs for the altar** to decipher it; others go to protect her.
- The **Priestess goes down; Nelgron revives her.**
- A **bell sounds** and the constructs surge again, downing several party members. **Nelgron runs between the fallen, reviving them and holding off the constructs.**
- **Altar text:**
  > *Before the gods named the heavens, / before the mountains learned to stand, / the First Fire burned without a keeper. / Seven hands bound what could not be destroyed.*
- **The three readings:**
  > **1.** *Seven memories became seven chains. / And the world endured. / But what is bound and not remembered / will one day be forgotten.*
  >
  > **2.** *The First Fire cannot be commanded. / It cannot be owned. / It cannot be fed with death alone. / Let the broken be acknowledged. / Let the bound be remembered. / Let the keeper choose.*
  >
  > **3.** *Speak what was lost. / Remember what was bound. / Give the Fire its name again.*
- When the last glyphs are read, the constructs return to their places and **the roof opens on five great chains**. This is consistent with two broken.

**Kaitlin's choice and the escape**
- **Kaitlin is offered the choice: hold the chains, or save herself.**
- **The Rogue shouts for her to save herself. She runs.**
- Another collapse follows, and the party runs and climbs out.
- **They emerge in the Adder Peaks** at night, before an immense black-stone fortress with glowing towers and smoking chimneys: **the Burning Anvil**.
  - The reference image showed spired citadels, colossal armored statues, lava aqueducts, an erupting volcano, and **crimson banners with a gold sigil**.

**Current position:** outside the Burning Anvil in the Adder Peaks.

## Open Threads (as of the end of "The Dawn Titans")

**The central mystery**
- **The First Fire and the seven bindings:** seven figures bound something primordial and elemental, "what could not be destroyed," in seven places. The bindings are made of memory: *"what is bound and not remembered will one day be forgotten."*
  - The erasure seen across the campaign (Shamph's blank archives, Mussum's forgetful monks, stripped memories, the lost weeks) appears to be the bindings failing.
- **Chains:** two of seven are broken (the broken-link chain, and the tower's chain during the fight with Zohir). Five remain. One was glowing.
- **The seven Keepers/lanterns:** six dark, one returned at Davarn. "Burned without a keeper" and *"let the keeper choose"* tie the keepers to the bindings.
- **"Remember us":** spoken by the memorial doors and by the Flayer's memory.
- **The readings as instructions:** *"Speak what was lost. Remember what was bound. Give the Fire its name again."* The Fire's name is unknown.

**Key NPCs**
- **Zohir:** the death-shrouded woman, faded away rather than killed. *"You think it was built to contain something…"* *"Cannot be fed with death alone"* may point at her.
