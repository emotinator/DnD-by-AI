## 1. Where they are

**Dawn, the morning after a long rest, at the rim of the steam cleft** in the Adder Peaks, 3–4 miles east of the basalt hollow along the gold seam. Fresh snow lies on the slopes; the cleft itself stays warm and smells of sulphur. The steam column roars without stopping. The ground pulses in **sets of five**, a few minutes apart and quickening.

Karrag-Dum is visible to the southwest, a few hours' walk down the hauling road. The stair into the hall is inside the ring of buried columns, broken two-thirds of the way down.

## 5. The fork, with light prep for each branch

### A. Down into the hall

**Getting down.** The stair is broken two-thirds of the way, and the ledge fell with the box. Options: hand over hand along the rope (Athletics DC 12; Arinir at disadvantage from his ribs if you rule the lingering injury), Nelgron's Slow Fall and speed, Paz flying a line down, or Melania/Valerian magic. The floor is about 15 ft below the break.

**What's there** (already established; see `bible.md` → the steam cleft):
- The **carved water channel**, boiling. Touching the water: 1d6 fire, or cross with a jump (Athletics DC 10).
- The **pillar and the visible gold chain**, one link cracked and steaming where the water pours on it.
- The **faceless figure** and the **empty lantern niche**. If Kaitlin brings the lantern near it, something should happen. *DM's choice*: it fits, it warms, or it shows something only in the Lantern of Revealing's light.
- The **frieze of seven faceless figures** with the angular script. **Melania's Helm of Comprehending Languages** reads it. *DM's choice what it says.* Suggested, in keeping with the theme: *"We gave our names to hold it. Remember us, and it stays."* (It explains why the faces were scraped: forgetting the seven weakens the chain.)
- **Teodar's lost box** lies in the channel, cooked.

**Danger.** Keep it light: the mephits were the guardians' playthings. Options if you want a beat of threat: more steam mephits drawn by noise; a shudder that sprays the hall with scalding steam (DC 12 Dex, 2d6 fire, half on a save); or the pulses visibly brightening the crack when Kaitlin is near.

**The Codex.** Valerian may want to sync here (Cartographer's Eye maps 60 ft; Primal Vulnerability). Each sync moves the **Eclipsed Page clock from 1 to 2**: the first signs (a pale raven, a torn page) appear soon after.

### B. Into Karrag-Dum with Teodar

**Getting in.** Teodar walks the hauling road every visit; surveyors and haulers are waved through at the lower gate. He can bring "assistants." Risks:
- **Kaitlin's face** is sketched on the Factor's wall and described in the lower markets. Disguise options: Melania's Trickery Domain magic, a hood and ash, or leaving her outside (she won't like being decided for).
- **The Mage** who led the avalanche ambush may know the party's faces.
- Teodar is **frightened**: he'll help, but he wants a promise that the Factor won't learn he fell.

**What Teodar can do:** get them to the embassy's back offices where he files his readings; point out the Factor's study (the sketch, the shipping manifests); arrange a meeting with the **hooded woman**, who he reports to separately and who pays well for news of the chain.

**The hooded woman.** *DM's choice who she is.* If Saressa: she knows the party through the coin, she wants the stations' power, and she's genuinely afraid of what's waking. She'd treat Kaitlin as either a key or a risk. Keep her intelligent and partly honest.

**The satchel.** If the party asks to see it: rising readings, a sketch map of the dig under the city, and *DM's choice* of one more page (a receipt for the hooded woman's payments, a note in the Factor's hand, or a list of sites with the star drawn on each).

### Either way

- **Valerian and Kaitlin** disagree (city vs. hall). Let the table settle it; neither is wrong, and both roads lead to the same truth.
- **The pulses quicken** every few hours. A soft clock, never stated as one.
