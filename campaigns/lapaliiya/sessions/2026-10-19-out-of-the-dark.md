# Session 2026-10-19 — Out of the Dark

_Arc 3 opener (mini-adventure). Session lead: Elmer. DM: Claude. Initiative: drama deck (Standard scene). Present: Nelgron, Hurintha, Melania, Arinir, Bubba Fatt, with Kaitlin. Absent: Valerian (separated in the Underdark), Theon and the Bard (back in a town, to be decided)._

## Recap
- The party climbed out of the collapsing lava tube onto a warm black-glass ledge in the Adder Peaks, at night, about 1–2 miles south of Karrag-Dum (the Burning Anvil). Kaitlin sat down and said nothing.
- They took a short rest at the tube mouth. Nelgron scouted alone (Perception 10).
- Nelgron found a warm, hidden hollow ringed by basalt columns about ¼ mile west, a switchback hauling road below (drag marks, snapped chain links), several melted circles in the snow, and a fresh column of steam and ash 3–4 miles east along the ridge.
- Every light in Karrag-Dum dimmed at once, then flared back, and the mountain shuddered.
- On her way back, four **magmins** clawed up out of the melted circles. Nelgron fought them alone and destroyed all four over four rounds, taking a few burns (fire resistance helped). Hits of note: two natural 20s; Tavern Brawler pushes kept the Death Bursts at arm's length; Hand of Healing mid-fight.
- In the soft rock of one circle she saw a **pale gold seam of light** pulse once, like a heartbeat, and fade east toward the steam column.
- **Kaitlin spoke for the first time since the altar:** "They came up when the mountain shook. I felt it. Here. Like something pulling." (fist under her breastbone)
- Nelgron took 4 warm cinders from the craters as bedroll stones.
- Back at camp, Nelgron examined the party. Medicine 19: **Arinir has two cracked ribs** (nothing punctured) from covering Kaitlin's escape. She used Hand of Healing on him (roll pending).
- The party moved to the basalt hollow and settled in for a long rest. The session ended before the night was run.

## Decisions and consequences
- Nelgron engaged the magmins rather than lead them to camp. The party slept through it; only Kaitlin saw.
- The party left the tube mouth for the hidden hollow. They're out of sight of the city.

## Rulings made
- **Max HP now includes Con modifier × level (2024 rules), party-wide.** Confirmed by the session lead. New maxima: Nelgron 38, Hurintha 55, Melania 38, Arinir 38, Bubba Fatt 36, Theon 28.
- **Tavern Brawler push before Death Burst:** the push resolves before the death explosion, so the burst goes off at the pushed position.
- **Putting out Burning in snow:** dropping prone and rolling in the snow puts it out without using an action (standing costs half movement).
- **Dodge after attacking:** the session lead ruled that Nelgron's "defensive position" in round 1 was a plain Dodge with no Focus spent, though her action had gone on attacks. Flagged for review: in 2024 that's Patient Defense's 1-Focus option. Later "defensive" calls were read as Patient Defense with 1 Focus.
- Magmins are immune to fire, so their Death Bursts don't chain through each other.
- **Hand of Healing in Flurry:** one Flurry of Blows strike swapped for Hand of Healing (2024 Warrior of Mercy).
- Deflect Attacks doesn't work on Death Burst (it's a save, not an attack, and fire damage).
