# Arc 1 · Session 2 — The Ember Throne

_Date played: ? · Session lead: previous DM · Party level: 2 · Players: 7_
_Source: Nelgron Illfigure's event log (chronicle Entry 22, "Ember Throne"), `archive/chronicles/nelgron-entry-22-ember-throne.md`. The previous DM's prep for this session is in `archive/prep/`; play went differently in several places. This file is final once the table confirms it._

## Recap

1. The party followed the captive "hawkling girl" into the Adder Peaks through knee-deep snow. When distant figures came into view, the party couldn't agree on what they were. The Goliath broke away for high ground alone and came back under arrow fire.
2. **Avalanche ambush.** An avalanche split the party, and the Druid's thunder spell set off a second slide. Nelgron crossed a fallen tree to reach the cut-off group, then dug the Druid out from among the enemy dead before the cold killed him. Two attackers died and two fled. The ambushers wore black and red, and a Mage was with them.
3. A note on the dead read: *"Transfer the hawkling asset before the ember feast."*
4. On the volcano slope, the party saw chained men hauling basalt by wagon, overseen by a small fire giant. They watched and moved on without stepping in.
5. They reached the fire giant city, with its black basalt spires, a temple of Surtur, and markets beside slave pens worked by Azer slavers. The Druid found a cooled lava tube, and the party used it as a hidden base for several days of scouting.
6. **The embassy.** Through the lava tube and the closed market, the party reached the embassy walls. They overheard the Mage from the ambush talking with a soft-spoken, well-dressed Halfling: once the girl was secured, the giants would enforce Amn's mining rights.
7. The Ranger and Druid got inside disguised as servants and brought out documents: fragments of a Council of Five treaty and a letter to a Factor (see Loot).
8. The Mage was overheard saying he wasn't sure he hadn't left her at Kiln Hall. The party went there.
9. **Kiln Hall.** Chain catwalks ran above a floor split by lava, with guards at the door, a Huge giant working at the center, and the captive held at the back wall. The party split between the catwalks and the door, and two Azer at the front made the fight dangerous fast.
   - The Priest went down near the lava gap.
   - The Huge giant turned on the smaller fire giant and smashed it, apparently a feud between the giants.
   - Nelgron came down a chain, striking the fire giant on the way. The Druid was hit from below and fell from the upper chain.
   - Nelgron reached the Priest and revived him, then was knocked unconscious himself.
10. **The carrying.** Nelgron came to as the party ran toward the Priest, who was chanting something old with a "moon" quality to it. Moonlight came through a window in the fire city, and the party was suddenly in Ormpetarr. No one can explain the transition.
11. **The captive came with them.** She was carried home with the party and has since joined the group. She is **not the real Princess Carlalyn Hawklin**: she was a decoy or impostor. Nelgron's log says the captive wasn't brought back; that's his mistaken memory, and the table's account is canon.

## Decisions and consequences
- **Didn't help the basalt slaves on the slope:** no consequence yet, but those workers are still out there.
- **Waited and scouted from the lava tube:** this got the party into the embassy and produced the documents.
- **Split up in Kiln Hall:** most of the party went down, but the captive was freed. She turned out to be a decoy.
- **The Priest's chant:** the party escaped, but the cost and the source of that power are unknown.

## NPCs met or changed
- **The Mage (black and red):** led the avalanche ambush, works at the Amnian embassy, and is unsure where the girl was held. He may recognize the party.
- **Soft-spoken Halfling:** well-dressed and senior at the embassy, possibly the Factor or someone working for him.
- **The Huge giant of Kiln Hall:** killed a smaller fire giant mid-fight. Identity unknown.
- **"Karalyn" (the decoy):** the captive held in Kiln Hall, rescued and now traveling with the party. She isn't the real princess. Who she really is, and whether she knew she was a decoy, is unknown.
- **The real Princess Carlalyn Hawklin:** never found. Still missing.
