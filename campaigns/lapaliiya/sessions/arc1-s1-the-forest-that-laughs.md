# Arc 1 · Session 1 — The Forest That Laughs

_Date played: ? · Session lead: previous DM · Party level: 2 · Players: 7_
_Source: Nelgron's event log, Part 2 ("Before the Woods" and "Chondalla Woods"), `archive/chronicles/nelgron-campaign-event-log-entries-1-31.md`. Replaces the earlier prep-only draft; play differed from the prep in most places. Final once the table confirms it._

## Recap

1. Fire giants took the princess again: thirty escorts dead, seven giants among them. City rumour says the giants were hired. Messengers asked for the party's help with no payment offered, and the party agreed.
2. Nelgron sold the party's loot using her old skills, getting a better-than-fair price.
3. The party was careless, buying obvious supplies and asking telling questions, and drew attention.
4. **Inn ambush:** oak timbers barred the doors from outside and the inn was set on fire. The party escaped and fought the attackers on the ramparts. One attacker died; the rest escaped. A note on the body read *"Forge Agreement… Mt. Aider contract."*
5. The party left the city before dawn under suspicion.
6. **The Chondalwood:** a hostile forest with shifting paths and disorientation. Drunk satyrs blocked the trail; the Priest negotiated passage, suspiciously easily.
7. The party camped without fire.
8. **Night ambush:** hooved wooden "puppets" controlled by a poisonous vine creature. The party won, badly battered.

## Decisions and consequences
- **Careless shopping:** drew the inn ambush.
- **Leaving before dawn:** the party left under suspicion.
- **Fireless camp:** safer, but the vine creature found them anyway.

## NPCs met or changed
- **The satyrs:** drunk; let the party pass easily. Why so easily is unexplained.
- **The inn attackers:** Amnian agents, by the note. Most escaped.

## Loot and milestones
