# House Rules

_Last updated: 2026-11-05 · Core settings (ruleset, leveling, initiative, lethality, tone, lines and veils) live in `campaign.md`. This file holds what the table adds on top._

## Table conventions
- **Earned level-ups.** A milestone comes with an in-fiction justification from the player: four "magnificent" feats, saves or actions drawn from the character's history. Both Nelgron's log and Melania's sheet notes mention it, so it looks table-wide.
- **Hit points: 2024 rules, Con modifier × level included** (confirmed 2026-10-19 by Elmer). D&D Beyond sheets in this campaign omit it, so add it.
- **Names are loose on purpose.** The table has a running joke of getting NPC names wrong, so spellings drift (Lapadiiya/Lapaliiya, Maetherwatch/Maerthwatch, Caralyn/Kaitlin). Use the bible's spelling in narration; don't correct players.
- **The party count is unreliable on purpose.** People vanish, are replaced, and come back. Don't reconcile it; ask the session lead who is present.
- **Some questions are never written down.** The Keeper of the Last Gate's question and each character's answer are kept only in the players' heads. Don't record them in any file.

## Initiative add-ons (Drama Deck)
_Adopted 2026-10-24. They give individual speed back its value under side initiative. The deck rules themselves are in `dm-core/modules/drama-deck.md`. "Initiative bonus" means the number on the character's D&D Beyond sheet._

- **Opening redraw (round 1 only).** When combat starts, compare the best initiative bonus among the PCs present with the best among the foes. After the first card is flipped, the side with the higher bonus may set it aside and draw again. **The second card stands** (no redraw of a redraw), and the set-aside card is out for the rest of the fight. Ties: no redraw. A surprised side can't use it.
- **Jump the line (once per combat, per character).** A character with initiative **+4 or better**, or the **Alert** feat, may act during the other side's phase, before anyone on it. Declare it after the card is flipped, before anyone acts. The jumper takes their whole turn first, and it replaces their turn in their own side's phase that round. Card effects on their own side still apply to them. If several jump in the same round, the players choose their order. Against a villain **Flurry**, the jumper acts before both villain turns.
- **Villains too.** A foe with initiative +4 or better may Jump the line once per combat; the DM announces it when it happens. Foes count for the Opening redraw as above.
- **Tracking:** the status block's WAITING line notes who has already jumped this fight.

Bracers of Defense with homebrew scaling (her log says +1; her D&D Beyond sheet applies the standard +2, giving AC 18): they grow only through sacrifice borne for those who cannot repay it, never through kills, and restore themselves when used to heal or tend the dying. Scaling steps aren't written down yet; the session lead decides when they grow.

### Codex of the First Root (Valerian) — DRAFT, under discussion (2026-11-01)
_The player brought an LLM-generated version; this is the campaign-tuned draft. Direction from the session lead: build it into backstory, **keep it light at the start**, let it grow with levels. Nothing here is final until the table agrees._

Wondrous item, requires attunement by a druid. Starts uncommon and grows with Valerian.
- **Already attuned.** The player's version had an earlier stage where using it cost HP ("hostile self-defense protocols"); treat that as backstory. The burn under his ribs was that stage.
- **Tier 1, awakened (now, level 3–4):**
  - **Star Map.** The Codex *is* his Circle of the Stars Star Map and spellcasting focus. No extra power, just flavour.
  - **Planar Data Sync**, **once per long rest**, as an action. Choose one:
    - **Cartographer's Eye:** learn the layout within **60 ft**, including hidden doors and passages and mechanical or magical traps (where they are, not how to bypass them). Replaces the original "exact layout of the current dungeon level."
    - **Primal Vulnerability:** pick a creature you can see within 60 ft. Learn its damage vulnerabilities, resistances and immunities; the **next attack roll** against it before the end of your next turn has **advantage**. Replaces the original "spell check," which isn't a rule.
  - **Life for Life.** Extra syncs beyond the free one cost **one Hit Point Die** each (rolled and lost, no healing), or **1 level of Exhaustion** if none remain.
  - **Every sync pings** (see the threat tracker below).
- **Tier 2, rooted (level 5 or a story beat):** 2 uses per long rest; range 120 ft; Primal Vulnerability as a bonus action.
- **Tier 3, flowering (level 9 or a major story beat), DM-side:** a new option, **Archive Query**: once per long rest, cast *Legend Lore* or *Commune with Nature* through the Codex without a slot. Its ping counts double.

**The Ping / Eclipsed Page threat tracker (draft).** A DM-side clock, **0–6**. **Current: 1** (set 2026-11-05: Valerian's journal says the Order's scanners were already sweeping the deep before he surfaced). No syncs yet. Each sync **+1**; a costed extra sync **+2**; **−1 per week** without a sync. Players see the signs, not the number (to confirm).
- **2 — signs:** a pale raven, a torn page left where he'll find it, a stranger asking after "the surveyor."
- **4 — a hunting party:** a scout team scaled to the party, sent to locate, not kill.
- **6 — an Elite Censor:** a set-piece encounter; afterwards the clock resets to 2.
- The Order itself is DM-only; see `bible.md` → Factions.

## Adventure-specific rules from prep (use if the place comes up again)
- **Twisted Path:** a long rest in an unprotected stretch gives no benefit; short rests work. Reality Twists (d6) and Spite's Whisper (Wisdom save or a compulsion to act against the apparent betrayer; the PC keeps agency and remembers it).
- **Moonlight vs. darkness (Selûnite sites):** in direct moonlight, Shadows can't hide; moonlit areas give +1 to saves.

## Rulings that stuck
- **Tavern Brawler push vs. death effects** (2026-10-19): the push resolves before a death effect like Death Burst, which then goes off at the pushed position.
- **Putting out Burning in snow** (2026-10-19): drop prone and roll; no action needed. Standing costs half movement.
- **Ready action = one attack** (2026-11-01): a readied attack is a single attack even with Extra Attack; Extra Attack applies only to the Attack action on your own turn.
- **Wolf fur as scent masking** (2026-11-01): working a pack's own shed fur into gear beat ash; with Survival 22 and the den returned plus a toll, pursuit ended outright.
- **Wild Companion (2024 druid)** (2026-11-01): spend a Wild Shape use to cast Find Familiar; seeing through its senses is a bonus action, and your own body is blinded and deafened meanwhile.
- **Death Burst vs. a restrained or pinned creature** (2026-11-01): its Dex save is at disadvantage.
- **Tavern Brawler push before Death Burst** applied again 2026-11-01 (both mephits shoved off the lip before bursting).
- **Under review: Aid** (2026-11-01): played as +8 HP. RAW is +5 with a 2nd-level slot, +10 with 3rd. Confirm the slot Paz used, or keep +8 as a table ruling.
- **Under review: Healing Word as one of two Flurry turns** (2026-11-01): Melania used one Drama Deck Flurry turn on Healing Word (normally a bonus action) and the other on Sacred Flame. No effect on the outcome; decide whether a bonus-action spell plus a cantrip could share one turn.
- **Deflect Attacks vs. area effects** (2026-10-19): doesn't apply to saving-throw effects (attack rolls only) or, before level 13, to non-B/P/S damage.
- **Under review:** in 2026-10-19 round 1, the lead allowed a Dodge after Nelgron's action went on attacks, with no Focus spent. In 2024 rules that's Patient Defense with 1 Focus. Decide which stands.
