# Campaign Settings — Lapaliiya

_Last updated: 2026-10-24 · The settings the DM core reads for this campaign. Change a setting here, not in `dm-core/instructions.md`._

## Identity
- **Name:** Lapaliiya (working title for the whole campaign)
- **Premise:** an escort job out of Lapaliiya became a kidnapping plot, then a descent into a failing ancient prison made of memory. Homebrew, built around published Forgotten Realms material.
- **Status:** in progress. Arc 2 ended; a mini-adventure on the Adder Peaks began 2026-10-19. See `log-recent.md`.
- **Setting:** Forgotten Realms: the Vilhon Reach, Chondath, the Adder Peaks, the Underdark.

## Rules
- **Ruleset:** D&D 5e (2024 PHB, DMG, MM).
- **Leveling:** milestone. Players earn a level with four "magnificent" feats, saves or actions from their character's history.
- **Party level:** mixed, 3–5 per D&D Beyond (see `roster.md`).
- **Hit points:** 2024 standard, including Con modifier × level (confirmed 2026-10-19). Sheets omit it; add it.
- **Initiative:** `drama-deck` (module: `dm-core/modules/drama-deck.md`), with the Opening redraw and Jump the line add-ons in `house-rules.md`.
- **Dice:** players roll their own (D&D Beyond or physical); DM rolls closed.

## Lethality and encounters
- **Default difficulty:** close to even, tilted slightly toward the heroes. Most fights leave most of the party hurt but standing.
- **Mix:** mostly even challenges; heroes-outclass-foes regularly; escalation chains across each arc; walk-away threats occasionally.
- **Character death:** possible, mainly from fights the party chose after clear warnings.
- **Table note:** this group sometimes gets overconfident. Use the one-cue warning before clearly lethal choices.

## Tone and content
- **Tone:** heroic fantasy, with mystery, memory and cosmic unease as recurring themes (forgetting, lost names, the chains below).
- **Lines (never):** to be decided. **Default:** nothing graphic.
- **Veils (fade to black):** to be decided.

## Table
- **Players:** usually 5–6, up to 8. The party count is unreliable on purpose (see `house-rules.md`).
- **Session leads:** Elmer, and rotating co-DMs.
- **Play-by-post:** Discord, pasted in with `DISCORD:`.
- **Status block:** on.
